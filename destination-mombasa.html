Welcome to the Kenyan coast

A city shaped by history, culture and the sea

Mombasa is one of Kenya's most fascinating coastal destinations. Its character has been shaped by centuries of Indian Ocean trade and cultural exchange, bringing together Swahili, Arab, Asian, Portuguese and other influences.

Wander through the narrow lanes of Old Town, discover the historic walls of Fort Jesus, enjoy the views at Mama Ngina Waterfront or head towards the northern and southern beaches for a relaxing Indian Ocean escape.

Whether you have a few hours for sightseeing or several days for a coastal holiday, Mombasa offers a rewarding mix of heritage, food, shopping, waterfront scenery and ocean activities.

Culture and local life

Experience Mombasa's Swahili heritage

Mombasa's culture is reflected in its architecture, language, food, markets and everyday life. A guided walk through Old Town offers opportunities to learn about the city's historic neighbourhoods and the communities that have shaped its identity.

Visit local markets, admire traditional architecture and discover the character of the coastal city at a comfortable pace.

  • Explore Old Town's historic streets
  • Discover local markets and crafts
  • Learn about Swahili architecture and traditions
  • Enjoy local food and coastal hospitality
Coastal scenery

Enjoy the Indian Ocean atmosphere

Mombasa's relationship with the ocean is part of its identity. From the harbour and Likoni Channel to the northern beaches, the city offers a variety of coastal landscapes and experiences.

Spend time by the water, enjoy a boat excursion or combine your city visit with a relaxing beach stay.

  • Take in views of the harbour and waterways
  • Enjoy selected boat and marine excursions
  • Relax at the northern coastline
  • Combine Mombasa with Diani, Watamu or Malindi

More Places to Discover

Extend your stay with nature, wildlife and coastal experiences around the greater Mombasa area.

Haller Park

Visit a nature sanctuary in Bamburi known for its restored landscape, nature trails and wildlife experiences. Check current visitor activities before travelling.

Mombasa Marine National Park

Discover the coastal marine environment through suitable boat trips, glass-bottom boat experiences and optional snorkelling, depending on sea conditions and operator arrangements.

Shimba Hills

Take a day excursion inland to explore forest scenery, wildlife habitats and the beautiful Sheldrick Falls area.

Wasini Island

Consider a longer coastal excursion from the south coast for boat experiences, marine scenery and a taste of island life.

North Coast

Explore Nyali, Bamburi and Shanzu for beach accommodation, restaurants, leisure activities and coastal relaxation.

South Coast and Diani

Extend your holiday towards Diani Beach for white sands, beach resorts and a wide range of ocean activities.

Planning a Visit to Mombasa

A little planning helps you make the most of your time on the Kenyan coast.

When to Visit

Mombasa is a year-round coastal destination with warm, humid conditions. Many visitors prefer the generally drier periods for sightseeing and beach activities, while the coast can also be enjoyed during wetter months.

Weather and sea conditions vary, so boat trips, snorkelling and other water activities should always be confirmed close to the travel date.

Getting Around

Mombasa can be explored using private transfers, taxis and local transport options. Travel times vary with traffic, road conditions and the location of your accommodation.

  • Moi International Airport serves Mombasa.
  • SGR services connect Mombasa with Nairobi.
  • Private transfers can be arranged in advance.
  • Allow extra time when travelling between the island and the mainland.

How Long Should You Stay?

A one-day visit can cover some of the major city landmarks. Two to three days allow more time for cultural sightseeing, waterfront visits and selected coastal activities.

A longer stay is ideal if you want to combine Mombasa with Diani, Watamu, Malindi or a wildlife safari.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable walking shoes for city sightseeing
  • Light clothing suitable for warm coastal weather
  • Sun protection, sunglasses and a hat
  • Swimwear for beach and marine activities
  • A camera and a reusable water bottle
