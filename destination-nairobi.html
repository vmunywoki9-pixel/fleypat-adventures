The city and beyond

Discover the many sides of Nairobi

Nairobi is Kenya's energetic capital and one of East Africa's most important urban centres. It combines modern business districts, lively neighbourhoods, cultural attractions and green spaces.

One of its most remarkable features is the presence of Nairobi National Park, a wildlife reserve located close to the city. This creates an unusual opportunity to combine a city stay with a safari experience.

Whether you are visiting for business, leisure or as part of a longer Kenyan holiday, Nairobi offers an excellent introduction to the country's people, history and natural heritage.

Explore the capital

Experiences to enjoy in Nairobi

Discover the city's landmarks, wildlife attractions, cultural heritage and green spaces through experiences suited to your interests and schedule.

Wildlife and city views

Nairobi National Park

Experience a wildlife safari with Nairobi's skyline visible beyond the park landscape. The park is known for its open grasslands and a variety of wildlife, although sightings are never guaranteed.

City landmark

KICC and City Centre

Explore the central business district and see the iconic Kenyatta International Convention Centre. Its rooftop viewpoint, when accessible, offers a memorable perspective of the capital.

History and heritage

Karen Blixen Museum

Visit the historic farmhouse associated with Danish author Karen Blixen. The museum provides insight into an important chapter of Kenya's colonial-era history.

Culture and learning

Nairobi National Museum

Explore Kenya's natural history, cultural heritage and artistic collections. It is a useful stop for visitors who want to understand more about the country's people and landscapes.

Green spaces

Uhuru Park

Enjoy one of Nairobi's best-known public green spaces near the city centre. It offers a different pace from the busy streets and views towards the surrounding urban skyline.

Nature in the city

Karura Forest Area

Discover Nairobi's greener side with a visit to Karura Forest. Its walking and cycling trails provide a refreshing outdoor experience within the city.

A safari within the capital

Experience Nairobi's remarkable wildlife

Nairobi National Park offers an unusual safari setting close to the capital's urban landscape. Its open plains and natural habitats support a range of wildlife, making it a popular excursion for visitors with limited time in Nairobi.

Wildlife experiences can be combined with other attractions around the city, depending on your available time and preferred pace.

Nairobi: Your gateway to Kenya's safaris

Nairobi is a major starting point for journeys to Kenya's national parks, reserves and coastal destinations. Travellers can begin with a city stay before continuing to the country's famous wildlife areas.

Fleypat Adventures can help coordinate a journey from Nairobi, including transport planning, accommodation arrangements and safari experiences based on your travel dates.

→Maasai Mara — famous for its open savannahs and seasonal wildlife migration.
→Amboseli — known for elephant herds and views of Mount Kilimanjaro when conditions are clear.
→Lake Nakuru — a Rift Valley destination known for birdlife and wildlife.
→Tsavo — expansive wilderness landscapes and distinctive red-earth scenery.

Before you travel

Plan your Nairobi visit

Nairobi can be explored as a short city break, a wildlife-focused visit or the first stop on a longer Kenyan holiday.

We can help coordinate your arrangements according to your arrival time, group size, interests and onward travel plans.

Getting There Nairobi is served by Jomo Kenyatta International Airport and Wilson Airport. Airport transfers can be arranged according to your arrival details.
Getting Around Private transfers and guided city transport can make it easier to visit attractions around the capital.
Wildlife Visits Park entry, conservation attraction access, visitor sessions and activity arrangements should be confirmed before travel.
Accommodation Choose from city hotels, boutique accommodation and other stays based on your preferred location and budget.
What to Pack Comfortable walking shoes, light layers, sun protection and a camera. A light jacket can be useful during cooler mornings and evenings.
Safari Planning If continuing beyond Nairobi, allow enough time for road transfers, park entry arrangements and your chosen itinerary.
