What information should you provide?

To help us understand your requirements, include as many travel details as possible when sending your enquiry.

  • Departure city or airport.
  • Destination city or airport.
  • Preferred departure date.
  • Return date, if applicable.
  • Number of adult and child passengers.
  • Preferred cabin class, if relevant.
  • One-way, return or multi-city journey.
  • Any important baggage or connection preferences.

Before you book

Important flight booking information

Understanding your ticket conditions helps you prepare for a smoother journey.

Flight Fares

Airfares may change depending on availability, travel dates, airline policies and booking conditions. A quoted fare is subject to confirmation at the time of booking.

Baggage Allowances

Checked and cabin baggage allowances differ by airline, route and fare type. Confirm your allowance before travel.

Passenger Details

Passenger names and travel document details must be accurate and consistent with the documents required by the airline and destination.

Changes & Cancellations

Changes, refunds and cancellations depend on the applicable airline and ticket fare rules. Some fares may have restrictions or additional charges.

Travel Documents

Travellers are responsible for checking passport validity, visa requirements, transit conditions and other entry requirements before departure.

Check-in & Departure

Check your airline's recommended airport arrival time, check-in deadlines, terminal details and boarding requirements before your journey.

Please note: Fleypat Adventures provides flight enquiry and ticketing assistance subject to the booking arrangements available through the relevant airline or ticketing provider. Flight schedules, fares and availability are not guaranteed until confirmed. Visa approvals and entry decisions remain the responsibility of the relevant authorities.
