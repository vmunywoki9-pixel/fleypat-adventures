The Experience

Discover the History and Flavours of Zanzibar

Zanzibar is an island destination where African, Arab, Indian and European influences come together. Its historic streets, traditional architecture, lively markets and spice-growing heritage create a distinctive cultural experience.

This three-day escape introduces you to the character of Stone Town and the island's famous spices. Enjoy guided exploration, discover local traditions and experience the relaxed atmosphere of the island.

Your Journey

3-Day Suggested Itinerary

Your programme can be adjusted according to arrival and departure times, accommodation location and preferred activities.

Day 1 · Arrival and Heritage

Arrival in Zanzibar & Stone Town Orientation

Arrive in Zanzibar and meet your local transfer representative for the journey to your accommodation. After check-in and some time to settle in, begin discovering the historic atmosphere of Stone Town.

Depending on your arrival time, enjoy an orientation walk through the old streets, discover the distinctive architecture and experience the local atmosphere around the town.

  • Arrival assistance and transfer to accommodation
  • Hotel check-in, subject to the property's check-in time
  • Optional guided Stone Town orientation
  • Free time to enjoy the evening at your own pace

Overnight: Zanzibar accommodation.

Day 2 · Spice and Culture

Spice Farm Experience & Island Exploration

After breakfast, head out for a guided spice experience. Visit a traditional spice-growing area and discover how Zanzibar earned its reputation as the Spice Island.

Learn about the uses and origins of different spices, experience their distinctive aromas and see how selected crops are grown and harvested. Continue with additional island exploration depending on your interests and available time.

  • Breakfast at the accommodation
  • Guided spice farm visit
  • Introduction to cloves, cinnamon, nutmeg and other spices
  • Optional local market or cultural stop
  • Return to accommodation

Overnight: Zanzibar accommodation.

Day 3 · Departure

Breakfast, Leisure & Departure

Enjoy breakfast and some final leisure time depending on your departure schedule. If time allows, you may enjoy a final walk around Stone Town, browse for local souvenirs or relax before your transfer.

  • Breakfast at the accommodation
  • Check-out according to hotel policy
  • Optional short town visit, subject to available time
  • Transfer to the airport or ferry terminal

Your Zanzibar escape concludes with onward travel arrangements confirmed according to your booking.

Before You Travel

Useful Travel Information

Getting to Zanzibar

Zanzibar can be reached by air or by ferry from mainland Tanzania. Your preferred arrival option, tickets and transfers should be confirmed before travel.

What to Pack

Pack comfortable walking shoes, lightweight clothing, sun protection and any personal essentials. Modest clothing is recommended when visiting religious and cultural sites.

Local Customs

Zanzibar has a rich Swahili and Islamic cultural heritage. Respect local customs, ask permission before photographing people and dress appropriately in town and religious areas.

Flexible Arrangements

The order of activities and timing may be adjusted according to arrival schedules, opening hours, local conditions and the availability of guides and service providers.
