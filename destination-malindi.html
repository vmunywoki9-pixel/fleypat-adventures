A different side of the coast

Discover the charm of Malindi

Malindi is one of Kenya's best-known coastal destinations, offering a blend of sandy beaches, marine experiences, historic landmarks and vibrant local life. Its Indian Ocean setting makes it an inviting place to slow down and enjoy the coast.

Beyond the beach, visitors can explore the town's Swahili and Portuguese-influenced heritage, discover the protected marine environment and enjoy the food, markets and welcoming atmosphere of the north coast.

Whether you are planning a relaxed beach holiday, a cultural escape or a coastal journey combining several destinations, Malindi offers plenty of ways to shape your stay.

Experiences to enjoy in Malindi

From the ocean and coral reefs to historic landmarks and relaxed coastal days, discover experiences that make Malindi memorable.

Ocean exploration

Malindi Marine Park

Explore the marine environment through activities such as snorkelling, boat excursions and reef viewing. Experiences depend on sea conditions, tides and the arrangements available on your travel date.

History and heritage

Vasco da Gama Pillar

Visit one of Malindi's most recognisable historic landmarks, set on a rocky point overlooking the Indian Ocean and connected to the town's long maritime history.

Local atmosphere

Markets and Coastal Life

Spend time discovering local shops, crafts and everyday coastal life. Markets offer an opportunity to appreciate the creativity and traditions of the region.

Coastal scenery

Oceanfront Moments

Enjoy the coastal atmosphere, sea views and changing colours of the Indian Ocean. A relaxed seaside day can be a welcome part of a longer beach holiday.

Swahili heritage

Historic Town Exploration

Discover the town's coastal architecture, religious landmarks and layered cultural influences. Visits to religious sites should be respectful of local customs and opening arrangements.

Beach relaxation

Sun, Sand and Sea

Make time for peaceful beach walks, ocean views and laid-back days by the water. Beach conditions can vary with tides, weather and the season.

Stories along the coast

A destination shaped by centuries of coastal history

Malindi has a long history as an Indian Ocean trading town. Its heritage reflects centuries of interaction between African coastal communities and visitors who travelled through the region by sea.

The town's historic sites offer an opportunity to understand its role in maritime trade and the cultural connections that shaped this part of the Kenyan coast.

01 Vasco da Gama Pillar
A landmark associated with the Portuguese voyages along the East African coast.
02 Portuguese Chapel
A historic site connected to Malindi's early Portuguese presence.
03 Swahili Coastal Culture
Discover local architecture, traditions, food and the town's distinctive coastal character.
