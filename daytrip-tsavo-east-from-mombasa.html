Discover Tsavo East

A Day in Kenya's Red-Earth Wilderness

Tsavo East is one of Kenya's largest national parks. Its open plains, seasonal water sources and distinctive red soil create a dramatic setting for a day of wildlife viewing.

Departing from the Mombasa coast, this safari offers an opportunity to explore the park with a professional driver-guide. Game viewing takes place from the safari vehicle, with time spent exploring suitable tracks and wildlife areas according to the day's conditions.

Please note: This is a long day trip involving an early departure and several hours on the road. The exact pickup time and route will be confirmed when your booking is arranged.

Your Day-by-Day Experience

1-Day Safari Itinerary

The schedule below is indicative. Actual departure, park entry and return times depend on your hotel location, road conditions and the agreed safari arrangements.

Early morning · Departure

Coastal Pickup and Journey to Tsavo East

Your driver-guide collects you from the agreed hotel or meeting point along the Mombasa coast. Travel inland towards Tsavo East, enjoying changing coastal and semi-arid scenery along the way.

  • Pickup from the confirmed location.
  • Drive towards the agreed Tsavo East entrance gate.
  • Brief orientation and park entry formalities.
Morning · Game drive

Wildlife Viewing in the Park

Begin your game drive through Tsavo East's open country and bushland. Your guide will look for elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelopes, buffaloes and other wildlife that may be in the area.

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, but your guide will use local knowledge and current conditions to plan the best available route.

Midday · Scenic stop

Aruba Dam and Lunch Break

If included in the agreed route, visit the Aruba Dam area and enjoy the scenery around this important water source. Wildlife and bird activity varies with the season and water levels.

Take your arranged lunch break at the designated location. The meal arrangements will be confirmed in your quotation.

Afternoon · Return journey

Final Game Viewing and Return to the Coast

Depending on the agreed schedule, enjoy additional game viewing before exiting the park. Begin the return journey to the coast and arrive at your hotel or agreed drop-off point later in the day.

Your guide will advise on the day's timing and any adjustments required because of road or park conditions.
