Discover Tsavo East

A Safari into Kenya's Wild Heart

Tsavo East National Park is one of Kenya's most remarkable wilderness destinations. Known for its open plains, striking red-earth landscapes and large elephant herds, the park offers an exciting safari experience for visitors travelling from the Kenyan coast.

This day trip is designed for travellers who want to experience the African bush without spending a night away from the coast. An early departure makes it possible to enjoy game viewing during the morning and afternoon before returning to Mombasa.

What Makes Tsavo East Special

Experience the landscapes, wildlife and distinctive character of one of Kenya's most celebrated national parks.

01 / WILDLIFE

The Famous Red Elephants

Look out for Tsavo's elephants, often appearing red because of the park's distinctive red soil that coats their skin during dust bathing.

02 / LANDSCAPE

Open Savannah & Wilderness

Search for elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, antelopes, lions and other wildlife. Sightings depend on natural conditions and animal movements.

Your Safari Schedule

Suggested 1-Day Tsavo East Itinerary

This is a demanding full-day excursion with substantial road travel. Departure and return times depend on your pickup point, traffic, park entry arrangements and wildlife conditions.

Very Early Morning · Approximately 4:30–5:30 AM

Pickup from Mombasa & Drive to Tsavo East

Your safari begins with an early pickup from your hotel or agreed meeting point in Mombasa. Meet your driver-guide and travel towards Tsavo East National Park.

The early departure helps maximise the time available for wildlife viewing. Your exact pickup time will be confirmed according to your accommodation and selected park entry point.

  • Pickup from the agreed coastal location
  • Meet your driver-guide and receive a safari briefing
  • Travel towards Tsavo East National Park
  • Short comfort stops as necessary
  • Proceed to the agreed park entrance
Morning · Subject to Arrival Time

Morning Game Drive

After completing park entry procedures, begin your first game drive through Tsavo East. Explore selected tracks and wildlife viewing areas with your driver-guide.

Tsavo East is especially known for its elephant population. You may also encounter giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, antelopes and other wildlife. Predators such as lions may be present but are not guaranteed to be seen.

  • Explore selected park tracks
  • Search for elephants and other large mammals
  • Enjoy scenic stops where permitted
  • Learn about the park's ecology and wildlife
  • Photograph the distinctive Tsavo landscape
Midday · Approximately 12:00–1:30 PM

Lunch & Relaxation Break

Take a break from the game drive and enjoy lunch at an agreed lodge, restaurant or designated location, depending on the package and arrangements.

This is an opportunity to relax, refresh and appreciate the surroundings before the afternoon wildlife drive.

  • Proceed to the agreed lunch location
  • Enjoy lunch if included in your package
  • Relax and prepare for the afternoon game drive
  • Follow all venue and park safety instructions
Afternoon · Subject to Park Exit Time

Afternoon Game Drive

Continue exploring the park during the afternoon safari session. Your guide will select suitable routes based on the available time, road conditions and wildlife activity.

Keep an eye out for elephants, giraffes, zebras and other animals as you enjoy the changing light across the savannah landscape.

  • Continue wildlife viewing in selected areas
  • Enjoy additional photography opportunities
  • Observe wildlife from the safari vehicle
  • Begin the journey towards the agreed exit gate
Late Afternoon & Evening

Exit the Park & Return to the Coast

After completing the safari, exit the park according to the applicable park hours and begin the return journey towards Mombasa or your agreed coastal drop-off location.

Arrival time will depend on the exit gate, distance, traffic and your final destination. This is a long day trip, so a later return should be expected.

  • Complete the final game drive
  • Exit the park within the permitted hours
  • Travel back towards the coast
  • Drop-off at the agreed location
  • End of your Tsavo East safari experience
Discover the Destination

Wildlife & Landscapes of Tsavo East

Tsavo East offers an expansive natural environment with varied habitats and a rich range of wildlife.

Elephants

Tsavo's elephants are among the park's most recognised wildlife attractions. The red appearance of many elephants comes from the iron-rich soil that coats their bodies.

Predators

Lions and other predators inhabit the park. Seeing them depends on wildlife movements, habitat conditions and the time spent searching in suitable areas.

Plains Game

Giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, antelopes and other herbivores contribute to the park's diverse wildlife experience.

Birdlife & Scenery

The park also supports a variety of bird species and offers striking views across open savannah, woodland and riverine habitats.

Before You Travel

Important Safari Information

Pickup Locations

Pickup can be arranged from Mombasa and other coastal locations by agreement. Diani, Watamu and Malindi pickups may require different departure arrangements and travel times.

Early Departure

This is a long-distance day safari. An early start is necessary to allow sufficient time for travel and game viewing. Your exact pickup time will be confirmed before travel.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable safari clothing
  • Closed shoes
  • Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Camera or binoculars
  • Drinking water
  • Valid identification and booking details

Wildlife Sightings

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. The animals encountered will depend on their natural movements, weather, habitat conditions and the duration of your game drives.

Park Regulations

Visitors must follow park rules, permitted driving hours, speed limits and ranger instructions. Animals should never be disturbed or approached outside authorised visitor arrangements.

Long Return Journey

The return drive can be lengthy. Actual arrival time depends on the park exit gate, road conditions, traffic and your final drop-off point. This excursion is best suited to travellers comfortable with a full day on the road.

Important booking note

Your final quotation will confirm the pickup and drop-off location, safari vehicle, driver-guide, park fees, meals, applicable taxes, payment arrangements and cancellation terms. The game-drive schedule may be adjusted to comply with park operating hours and the time available after the road transfer.
