Designed around your group

Travel arrangements that fit your plans

Every group has different priorities. Some need efficient business travel, while others want a relaxed group holiday or a combination of meetings and leisure activities.

When you contact us, sharing the following information helps us understand your requirements:

  • Organisation or group name
  • Number of travellers
  • Destination or preferred destinations
  • Proposed departure and return dates
  • Accommodation preferences
  • Transport and activity requirements
  • Estimated budget per person or for the group
Frequently asked questions

Corporate & Group Travel FAQs

Some useful information before you start planning.

Can you organise travel for large groups?

Yes. Share your estimated group size, destination and dates so we can discuss the arrangements and supplier options available for your group.

Can you arrange flights and accommodation together?

We can assist with flight search and ticketing enquiries as well as hotel and accommodation arrangements, subject to availability and confirmation.

Do you organise corporate retreats and team-building trips?

We can help explore suitable destinations, accommodation, transport and leisure experiences for corporate retreats and team outings.

Can we request a customised group itinerary?

Yes. Tell us your preferred destinations, travel dates, group interests and budget expectations so we can prepare a proposed itinerary for consideration.

How early should we make an enquiry?

We recommend enquiring as early as possible, especially for larger groups, peak travel periods and events with fixed dates. This gives more time to explore suitable options.

Planning a trip for your team or organisation?

Tell us about your group, destination and travel dates. Our team will be happy to discuss your requirements and help you explore suitable arrangements.
