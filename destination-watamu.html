Welcome to the Kenyan north coast

A coastal escape surrounded by nature

Watamu is a relaxed seaside destination in Kilifi County, north of Mombasa. It is known for its beautiful beaches, marine environment, coral reefs and the natural landscapes surrounding Mida Creek.

The destination offers a wonderful combination of beach relaxation, water-based activities, nature excursions and cultural discovery. Visitors can spend time by the ocean, explore marine habitats, take a boat trip through mangrove waterways or discover nearby historical sites.

Watamu is particularly appealing to travellers who enjoy coastal scenery, marine wildlife and quieter experiences away from larger urban centres.

Mangroves and birdlife

Discover the beauty of Mida Creek

Mida Creek is one of the most distinctive natural attractions around Watamu. Its tidal channels and mangrove ecosystems provide important habitats for birds and other wildlife.

Visitors can explore the creek through locally arranged boat excursions, guided nature experiences and visits to designated boardwalk areas.

  • Enjoy scenic mangrove waterways
  • Look out for local and migratory birdlife
  • Discover the coastal wetland ecosystem
  • Consider a locally arranged sunset dhow experience
Nature and conservation

Explore Watamu's coastal ecosystems

Watamu's natural environment extends beyond its beaches. Coral reefs, mangroves, tidal flats and nearby coastal forests support a variety of marine and terrestrial wildlife.

Nature-focused visits provide an opportunity to understand conservation work and the importance of protecting these habitats for future generations.

Planning a Visit to Watamu

A few practical considerations can help you enjoy a smooth and memorable coastal holiday.

When to Visit

Watamu can be visited throughout the year. Many travellers prefer generally drier periods for beach activities and marine excursions, although weather conditions can vary.

Sea conditions, visibility and tides influence water-based activities. Confirm the suitability of marine excursions close to your travel date.

Getting to Watamu

  • Malindi Airport is one option for reaching the region.
  • Moi International Airport in Mombasa is another arrival option.
  • Road transfers can be arranged from Mombasa and Malindi.
  • Private transfers are useful for travellers with luggage or groups.
  • Allow time for road travel when planning connections.

How Long Should You Stay?

Three to four days allow time for beach relaxation and one or two selected excursions.

Five to seven days provide more flexibility for marine activities, Mida Creek, Gede Ruins and nearby destinations.

What to Pack

  • Light and comfortable coastal clothing
  • Swimwear and beachwear
  • Sun protection, hat and sunglasses
  • Comfortable footwear for nature excursions
  • Reusable water bottle
  • Personal medication and travel essentials
