Discover the Pearl of Africa

Experience Uganda's remarkable natural beauty

Uganda is a destination of remarkable contrasts. Its landscapes include tropical rainforests, open savannahs, freshwater lakes, mountain ranges and the great River Nile.

The country is especially celebrated for mountain gorilla trekking in Bwindi Impenetrable National Park, wildlife experiences in Murchison Falls and Queen Elizabeth National Parks, and its beautiful western highlands.

Whether you are interested in nature, wildlife, culture or a wider East African journey, Fleypat Adventures can help you plan an itinerary around your travel preferences.

Explore Uganda

Experiences to make your journey memorable

Explore some of Uganda's best-known natural attractions. Each destination offers a different way to experience the country's landscapes, wildlife and cultural heritage.

Wildlife encounter

Bwindi Impenetrable National Park

Discover the forest habitat of endangered mountain gorillas. Gorilla trekking is a specialist guided experience that requires advance planning and a valid permit.

Natural wonder

Murchison Falls National Park

Witness the Nile as it forces its way through a narrow rocky gorge to create the dramatic Murchison Falls. The park also offers wildlife viewing and scenic river experiences.

Water-based safari

Kazinga Channel

Enjoy a boat excursion along the channel connecting Lake Edward and Lake George. The waterway is known for hippos, waterbirds and wildlife along its banks.

Wildlife & scenery

Queen Elizabeth National Park

Explore a diverse landscape of savannah, wetlands, crater lakes and forest habitats. The park offers game drives and opportunities to explore the Kazinga Channel.

Scenic landscapes

Western Uganda's Lakes

Discover beautiful lakes and changing landscapes in western Uganda. The region offers scenic viewpoints and opportunities to appreciate the country's natural diversity.

Forest adventure

Bwindi Forest Experience

Experience the atmosphere of one of Africa's most important mountain forest habitats. Forest walks and gorilla trekking arrangements depend on permitted activities and availability.

A special wildlife experience

Discover the mountain gorillas of Bwindi

Bwindi Impenetrable National Park is one of the most recognised destinations for mountain gorilla trekking. Its ancient forest provides habitat for mountain gorillas and many other species.

Gorilla trekking involves hiking through forest terrain with authorised guides and trackers. The duration and difficulty of the trek can vary depending on the location of the gorilla group and the day's conditions.

  • Advance gorilla permit arrangements are essential.
  • Permits are subject to availability and official requirements.
  • Physical preparation and suitable walking equipment are recommended.
  • Follow ranger instructions and wildlife viewing rules throughout the experience.
