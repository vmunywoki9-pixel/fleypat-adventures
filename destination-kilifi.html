A quieter coastal escape

Discover the charm of Kilifi

Kilifi is a coastal town between Mombasa and Malindi, known for its creek, beaches and relaxed atmosphere. It offers a different kind of coastal holiday for travellers looking to enjoy the sea without the pace of larger resort centres.

The town's waterfront setting is one of its defining features. Kilifi Creek reaches inland from the Indian Ocean, creating beautiful views and opportunities to enjoy the water and surrounding landscape.

Whether you are planning a peaceful couple's escape, a family break or a group getaway, Kilifi can be a rewarding place to slow down and enjoy Kenya's coast.

The heart of the destination

Experience the beauty of Kilifi Creek

Kilifi Creek is one of the area's defining natural features. Its sheltered waters, green shoreline and connection to the Indian Ocean give the destination a distinctive character.

It is a lovely setting for scenic moments, photography and selected water-based experiences. Depending on your plans, your stay can include time by the waterfront or an arranged excursion with a local operator.

History and culture

Discover Kilifi's coastal heritage

Kilifi's story extends beyond its beaches and creek. The wider area has a rich history shaped by coastal communities, Indian Ocean trade and centuries of cultural exchange.

The Mnarani archaeological site is one of the area's important heritage attractions. Its remains include historic settlement structures and the ruins of a mosque, offering a glimpse into the region's past.

A visit can add a cultural dimension to a coastal holiday, especially for travellers interested in history and the Swahili coast.

Visits to heritage sites should be arranged with attention to access, opening arrangements and any applicable entry requirements. We can help confirm practical details before your trip.
