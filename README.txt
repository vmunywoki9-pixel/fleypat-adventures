FLEYPAT ADVENTURES — FINAL WEBSITE PACKAGE

UPLOAD
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Upload every file to your hosting public_html / website root.
3. Keep all HTML files, styles.css and script.js together in the same folder.
4. Upload your own photos into this SAME folder as index.html. Do not create a separate images folder.
5. Upload your logo as fleypat-logo.png in the same folder.
6. Open your domain and test the navigation, contact form and WhatsApp links.

CUSTOMER ENQUIRY FORM
The contact form uses FormSubmit's AJAX endpoint to submit enquiries in the background to info@fleypatadventures.com. Customers stay on your website and do not need to open an email app. FormSubmit requires the mailbox owner to confirm the receiving address the first time the form is used. The first test submission may trigger an activation email; confirm it, then test again. Check the inbox and spam folder. The website does not contain your email password or SMTP credentials.
