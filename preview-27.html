The Experience

A Wildlife Adventure Just Outside Nairobi

Nairobi National Park offers a remarkable opportunity to experience Kenya's wildlife against the backdrop of a modern city. Its open grasslands, wooded areas and seasonal wetlands support a variety of wildlife, including rhinos, lions, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and numerous bird species.

This day trip combines a guided game drive in the national park with a visit to the Giraffe Centre in Lang'ata. It is a convenient experience for visitors with limited time in Nairobi, families, business travellers and anyone looking for a memorable wildlife day.

Your Day in Nairobi

Suggested Full-Day Itinerary

The following is an indicative schedule. Actual departure, activity and return times will be adjusted according to your pickup location, traffic, park conditions and visitor arrangements.

Early Morning · Approximately 5:30–6:00 AM

Pickup & Departure for Nairobi National Park

Your day begins with pickup from your Nairobi hotel, residence or another agreed meeting point. Meet your driver-guide and travel towards Nairobi National Park for an early start.

The park opens at 6:00 AM. After completing the entry arrangements, begin your game drive through the park's different habitats.

  • Pickup from the agreed Nairobi location
  • Meet your driver-guide and receive a trip briefing
  • Transfer to the national park
  • Complete park entry procedures
  • Begin the morning game drive
Morning · Approximately 6:30–10:30 AM

Wildlife Game Drive in Nairobi National Park

Spend the morning exploring Nairobi National Park in search of its wildlife. Your guide will help identify animals, explain their behaviour and share information about the park's habitats and conservation efforts.

Depending on the day and wildlife movements, you may have opportunities to see giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, antelopes, rhinos and other animals. Lions and other predators may also be encountered, but sightings are never guaranteed.

  • Explore selected park tracks and viewing areas
  • Look out for large mammals and birdlife
  • Enjoy wildlife photography opportunities
  • Learn about the park's ecosystems and conservation
  • Follow park rules and remain in the vehicle unless in designated areas

The duration of the game drive will depend on the selected package, park conditions and your preferred schedule.

Late Morning · Approximately 10:30 AM–12:30 PM

Exit the Park & Lunch Break

After the game drive, exit the park and proceed towards the next part of your experience. Depending on your package and schedule, enjoy lunch at an agreed restaurant or arrange a convenient meal stop.

This is also an opportunity to relax before continuing to the Giraffe Centre in Lang'ata.

  • Complete the game drive and exit the park
  • Proceed towards the agreed lunch location
  • Enjoy lunch if included in your selected package
  • Continue to the Giraffe Centre
Afternoon · Approximately 1:00–3:00 PM

Visit the Giraffe Centre

Continue to the Giraffe Centre in Lang'ata for an educational wildlife experience focused on the endangered Rothschild's giraffe.

Learn about giraffe conservation, observe the animals at close range and participate in the visitor experience permitted by the centre. The visit may also include educational information about giraffes and their habitat.

  • Arrive at the Giraffe Centre
  • Complete visitor entry arrangements
  • Learn about Rothschild's giraffe conservation
  • Enjoy the visitor experience and photography
  • Allow approximately 1.5–2 hours for the visit

The centre's visitor activities and access arrangements are subject to its current rules and operating conditions.

Afternoon · Approximately 3:00–4:30 PM

End of Experience & Return Transfer

After your visit to the Giraffe Centre, meet your driver for the return transfer to your hotel, residence or another agreed drop-off point in Nairobi.

Your day concludes with memories of Nairobi's wildlife and conservation experiences.

  • Depart the Giraffe Centre
  • Return transfer to the agreed drop-off point
  • End of the day trip
Wildlife Conservation

Discover the Giraffe Centre

The Giraffe Centre is located in Nairobi's Lang'ata area and is dedicated to the conservation of the endangered Rothschild's giraffe.

Visitors can learn about giraffe conservation, enjoy an educational experience and discover more about the work being undertaken to protect this distinctive species.

The centre recommends allowing approximately 1.5 to 2 hours for a fulfilling visit. Its published visitor hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, subject to any changes announced by the centre.

Before You Travel

Important Travel Information

Starting Point

The trip starts in Nairobi. Pickup is arranged from your hotel, residence, airport-area accommodation or another agreed location. Confirm your exact pickup point when booking.

Park Opening Hours

Nairobi National Park's published entry hours are 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Visitors should follow the park's entry and exit requirements and all ranger instructions.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable clothing and walking shoes
  • Sun protection, sunglasses and a hat
  • Camera or smartphone
  • Drinking water
  • Valid identification and booking details

Wildlife Viewing

Wildlife sightings depend on natural animal movements, weather and the time spent in the park. No particular animal sighting can be guaranteed.

Park Safety Rules

Remain inside the vehicle except in designated areas, follow the speed limit, avoid disturbing animals and follow instructions from your guide and park authorities.

Admission and Payments

Park and Giraffe Centre admission charges may vary according to visitor category and current rates. Confirm the applicable fees and payment arrangements before travel.

Important booking note

Your final written quotation will confirm the transport, vehicle arrangement, driver-guide services, park entry fees, Giraffe Centre admission, meals, applicable taxes, payment arrangements and cancellation terms. The itinerary may be adjusted according to traffic, visitor arrangements, park conditions and the centre's operating requirements.
