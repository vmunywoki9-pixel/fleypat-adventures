Accommodation across Kenya

Stay close to the experiences you want

Accommodation planning is about more than choosing a room. The location of your stay can affect your transfers, safari schedule, activities and overall travel experience.

We can help you consider accommodation in destinations such as:

  • Diani and the South Coast: beach resorts and coastal retreats.
  • Watamu and Malindi: beach properties and relaxed coastal stays.
  • Maasai Mara: safari camps and lodges.
  • Amboseli and Tsavo: lodges and camps suited to safari routes.
  • Nairobi and Mombasa: city hotels and convenient stopover accommodation.
  • Naivasha and Nakuru: lakeside and countryside stays.
