Discover the Wild Beauty of Samburu

Located in northern Kenya, Samburu National Reserve offers a distinctive safari experience shaped by semi-arid landscapes, rocky hills, open plains and the life-giving Ewaso Ng'iro River.

The river attracts wildlife to its banks, creating excellent opportunities to observe animals in a beautiful natural setting. The reserve is especially known for several northern Kenya species that are less commonly encountered in many of the country's other safari destinations.

From the striking patterns of Grevy's zebra to the long neck of the gerenuk, Samburu offers wildlife photography and game-viewing experiences with a character of its own.

Wildlife encounters

What to Experience in Samburu

Samburu is famous for its unique dry-country wildlife, beautiful river scenery and rewarding game drives.

01 · SPECIAL NORTHERN SPECIES

Grevy's Zebra

Recognisable by their narrow stripes, large rounded ears and distinctive appearance, Grevy's zebras are among Samburu's most celebrated wildlife species.

02 · DISTINCTIVE WILDLIFE

Reticulated Giraffes

Look for the beautifully patterned reticulated giraffe, a striking northern Kenya species often seen browsing on acacia trees.

03 · RIVER WILDLIFE

Elephant Encounters

Elephants are often associated with Samburu's river environment. Watch for herds moving between feeding areas and the river.

Samburu's special wildlife

Look Out for the Samburu Special Five

Samburu is widely associated with five distinctive northern Kenya species often grouped together as the Samburu Special Five. They are a major attraction for visitors interested in wildlife photography and dry-country ecology.

Grevy's zebraNarrow black-and-white stripes
Reticulated giraffeDistinctive net-like markings
Beisa oryxLong, straight horns
GerenukLong-necked antelope
Somali ostrichBlue-grey neck and legs in males

These species are adapted to the drier northern environment. Sightings depend on wildlife movements and conditions during your visit.

Beyond the special five

More Wildlife to Discover

Samburu's varied habitats support a wider range of mammals and birds, making every game drive an opportunity to discover something new.

PREDATORS

Lions and Other Big Cats

Lions and other predators inhabit the reserve. Your guide can help search suitable habitats while respecting wildlife viewing distances.

ANTELOPE

Greater Kudu

These elegant antelopes are known for their impressive spiral horns and are among the interesting species that may be encountered in the reserve.

BIRDLIFE

Birdwatching

Explore the reserve's birdlife around riverine vegetation, open country and other habitats. Bring binoculars for a closer look.

The heart of the reserve

Explore the Ewaso Ng'iro River

The Ewaso Ng'iro River is a defining feature of Samburu's landscape. Its waters support vegetation and wildlife in an otherwise dry environment, making the riverbanks important areas for animal activity.

During a game drive, you may see elephants approaching the water, birds resting along the banks and other animals moving through the surrounding vegetation.

The scenery also creates beautiful opportunities for photography, particularly when the light changes in the early morning and late afternoon.

Before you travel

Planning Your Samburu Safari

We help organise the practical details so your safari fits your dates, preferred comfort level and travel plans.

Getting There

Samburu is in northern Kenya. Access can be arranged by road or air depending on your itinerary, starting point and available transport options.

Best Time to Visit

Samburu can be visited throughout the year. Wildlife movements, seasonal rainfall and road conditions can influence the experience.

What to Bring

Pack comfortable safari clothing, a hat, sunscreen, sunglasses, drinking water, binoculars and a camera. A light layer may be useful during cooler mornings.

Safari Duration

A multi-day stay is useful for exploring the reserve at a relaxed pace. The ideal duration depends on your wider itinerary and interests.

Accommodation

Accommodation can be discussed according to your preferred comfort level, budget, location and availability for your travel dates.

Park Entry and Fees

Applicable entry fees, access requirements and operating arrangements should be checked and confirmed for your travel date before departure.

Wildlife sightings are not guaranteed. Animal movements and visibility vary with season, weather and natural conditions. Park access, fees, accommodation and transport arrangements are subject to confirmation at the time of booking.
