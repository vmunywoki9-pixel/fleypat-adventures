Discover Tsavo

Two Parks. One Remarkable Safari.

Tsavo East and Tsavo West together form one of Kenya's best-known wilderness regions, offering varied landscapes, wildlife encounters and memorable safari experiences.

Tsavo East is famous for its open plains, expansive horizons and red-dust landscapes. Its elephants are often seen with reddish soil on their bodies, giving them the familiar appearance of Tsavo's red elephants.

Tsavo West offers a different atmosphere, with rugged terrain, volcanic features, natural springs and diverse habitats. Mzima Springs is one of the area's best-known attractions, subject to access, timing and the day's itinerary.

This three-day safari combines both parks, giving you the opportunity to experience their contrasting scenery and search for wildlife with your driver-guide.

Two Distinct Wilderness Areas

What Makes This Safari Special?

Experience the contrasting character of Tsavo East and Tsavo West during one carefully planned safari.

01 · Tsavo East

Red-Earth Landscapes and Elephant Country

Tsavo East is known for its broad open spaces, red soil, seasonal rivers and large elephant population. Its landscapes provide an excellent setting for game drives and wildlife photography.

02 · Tsavo West

Springs, Volcanic Scenery and Rugged Terrain

Tsavo West has a more varied landscape, including rocky outcrops, volcanic formations and the natural waters of Mzima Springs. The park offers a different perspective on Kenya's wilderness.

Your Safari Journey

Detailed 3-Day Safari Itinerary

The following programme is a suggested route from Mombasa. Actual departure times, accommodation and park activities will be confirmed according to your travel dates and booking.

Day 1 · Mombasa to Tsavo East

Journey into Tsavo East National Park

Your safari begins with pickup from your agreed location in Mombasa. Meet your driver-guide and depart for Tsavo East National Park, travelling inland towards one of Kenya's largest protected wilderness areas.

On arrival, complete the park entry arrangements and begin your first game drive. Explore the park's open landscapes and search for elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffalo, antelopes and other wildlife.

The day's game viewing may include areas around the Aruba Dam, subject to the selected route, access and prevailing conditions. The dam and surrounding habitats can attract wildlife, particularly when water is limited elsewhere.

Later, proceed to your selected safari lodge or camp for dinner and overnight accommodation.

✓ Mombasa pickup ✓ Tsavo East game drive ✓ Dinner and overnight stay
Day 2 · Tsavo East to Tsavo West

Explore Tsavo West and Discover Mzima Springs

Start the day with breakfast before heading out for an early morning game drive in Tsavo East. Morning hours provide an opportunity to explore the park while many animals are active.

Return for breakfast or continue with the agreed morning arrangements before travelling towards Tsavo West National Park.

Depending on your selected route and available time, visit Mzima Springs, a natural oasis known for its clear spring water, aquatic habitats and surrounding vegetation. The area is associated with hippos, crocodiles and a variety of birdlife.

Continue exploring Tsavo West's diverse scenery before proceeding to your selected lodge or camp for dinner and overnight accommodation.

✓ Morning game drive ✓ Transfer to Tsavo West ✓ Mzima Springs if included ✓ Dinner and overnight stay
Day 3 · Tsavo West to Mombasa

Final Game Drive and Return to Mombasa

Enjoy breakfast before beginning your final morning game drive in Tsavo West, depending on your departure schedule and the accommodation location.

Explore the park's rugged scenery and look out for wildlife such as elephants, giraffes, zebras, buffalo and other animals. If the route and schedule allow, the day's sightseeing may include views of the volcanic landscapes around Shetani Lava Flow.

After the morning activities, begin the return journey towards Mombasa. Enjoy the changing scenery along the route before being dropped off at your agreed location.

✓ Breakfast ✓ Final game drive ✓ Return transfer to Mombasa

Before You Travel

Useful Safari Information

What to Pack

Prepare for Your Safari

Bring comfortable clothing, walking shoes, a hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, insect repellent, personal medication and a camera. A light jacket may be useful during cooler early mornings.

Wildlife Viewing

Enjoy the Experience Responsibly

Wildlife sightings vary with season, weather and animal movements. Follow your guide's instructions, maintain a respectful distance from animals and observe all park regulations.
