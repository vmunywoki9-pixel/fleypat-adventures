Discover the Maasai Mara

Experience the magic of the African savannah

The Maasai Mara National Reserve in south-western Kenya is one of Africa's most celebrated safari destinations. Its sweeping grasslands, seasonal rivers and rich wildlife populations create an exceptional setting for an African safari.

The reserve is especially known for its big cats, including lions, cheetahs and leopards, as well as elephants, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest and numerous other species.

This three-day experience takes you from Nairobi to the Mara for two nights of accommodation and exciting wildlife viewing. The programme can be adjusted to suit your travel dates, accommodation preference and budget.

  • Explore the famous Maasai Mara ecosystem
  • Search for lions, cheetahs and other predators
  • Observe elephants, giraffes, zebras and antelopes
  • Enjoy morning and afternoon game drives
  • Experience beautiful open savannah landscapes
  • Choose accommodation to suit your budget

Your Safari Journey

Detailed 3-Day Itinerary

Enjoy a carefully planned journey from Nairobi to the Maasai Mara. This is a suggested programme; exact departure times, game-drive schedules and meal arrangements will be confirmed in your quotation.

Day 01 · Nairobi to Maasai Mara

Journey to the African Wilderness

Your safari begins with an early morning pickup from your agreed location in Nairobi. Meet your driver-guide and begin the journey towards the Maasai Mara through Kenya's scenic Great Rift Valley region.

Enjoy views of the countryside, local settlements and changing landscapes along the route. There may be an opportunity for a short stop along the way, depending on the agreed travel arrangements.

Arrive in the Maasai Mara area around lunchtime, subject to road and travel conditions. Proceed to your selected lodge or tented camp for check-in and lunch according to the property's meal schedule.

In the afternoon, head out for your first game drive. Explore the open plains in search of the Mara's wildlife. Your guide will help you look for elephants, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest, buffaloes and predators that may be active.

Return to your accommodation in the evening for dinner and your first overnight stay in the Mara.

Day 02 · Full Day in the Maasai Mara

Full-Day Wildlife Exploration

Wake up early and enjoy breakfast before setting out for a morning game drive. The early hours offer an excellent opportunity to explore the reserve while wildlife is often active across the plains.

Travel through different habitats in search of the animals that make the Mara famous. Your guide will help you look for lions, cheetahs, elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeest and a variety of antelopes.

Depending on the selected safari arrangement, you may return to the lodge or camp for lunch and a short rest, followed by an afternoon game drive.

Alternatively, a full-day game drive with a picnic lunch can be arranged where suitable and permitted by reserve rules and your accommodation arrangements.

During the afternoon, continue exploring the savannah and riverine areas. The Mara River and its surrounding habitats are important wildlife areas, although the location visited will depend on the day's route and prevailing conditions.

Return to your lodge or camp in the evening for dinner and your second overnight stay.

Day 03 · Maasai Mara to Nairobi

Final Game Drive and Return Journey

Enjoy an early breakfast at your lodge or camp. Depending on your departure schedule and the accommodation's arrangements, you may have an opportunity for a final morning game drive.

Enjoy the last moments in the Mara, taking in the open grasslands and looking out for wildlife before returning to the lodge or camp to check out.

Begin your return journey to Nairobi. Travel through the scenic countryside and enjoy the changing landscapes along the way.

On arrival in Nairobi, you will be dropped off at your agreed location, marking the end of your three-day Maasai Mara safari.

Before You Travel

Important Safari Information

A few useful details to help you prepare for your Maasai Mara experience.

Departure and Pickup

This itinerary is designed to start in Nairobi. Pickup arrangements, departure time and the exact meeting point will be agreed before travel.

What to Pack

Bring comfortable clothing, closed shoes, a hat, sunscreen, sunglasses, personal medication, a camera and binoculars if available.

Best Time to Visit

The Mara offers wildlife viewing throughout the year. July to October is generally associated with the Great Migration in the Kenyan Mara, although timing and animal movements vary.

Wildlife Sightings

The Maasai Mara is rich in wildlife, but animals move freely in their natural habitat. Specific sightings, including big cats and river crossings, cannot be guaranteed.

Weather and Clothing

Weather conditions can vary. Pack light layers for early morning game drives and comfortable clothing for daytime activities.

Park Rules and Safety

Follow your guide's instructions, remain inside the safari vehicle unless advised otherwise, and respect wildlife and reserve regulations.
