Frequently asked questions

Private Safari Transport FAQs

Helpful information before making your transport enquiry.

Can I request a private safari vehicle?

Yes. You can enquire about private safari transport for your planned journey. Share your group size, dates and destinations so the arrangements can be discussed.

Can I arrange transport for a multi-day safari?

You can request transport arrangements for a multi-day safari itinerary. Include all the destinations you intend to visit when submitting your enquiry.

Can I request transport for a family or group?

Yes. Let us know how many people will be travelling and whether you have any particular luggage or travel requirements. Vehicle suitability will need to be confirmed when your enquiry is reviewed.

Do you arrange game-drive transport?

You can enquire about game-drive transport as part of your safari arrangements. Share the park or reserve you plan to visit and your preferred travel dates.

How much does private safari transport cost?

The cost depends on your route, travel dates, duration, group size and requested arrangements. Contact us for a quotation based on your itinerary.

Can my safari start from Nairobi?

You can enquire about safari transport starting from Nairobi. Include your preferred departure point and destination when contacting our team.
