Welcome to the Kenyan Coast

Discover the beauty of Diani Beach

Located along Kenya's beautiful south coast, Diani Beach is celebrated for its white sandy shoreline, warm tropical climate and clear Indian Ocean waters.

This four-day coastal escape is designed for travellers seeking relaxation, beach experiences and the freedom to enjoy the coast at their own pace.

Spend your days relaxing by the ocean, enjoying the tropical surroundings or adding optional marine and cultural excursions to your holiday.

  • Stay in the beautiful Diani Beach area
  • Enjoy white sandy beaches and ocean views
  • Optional snorkelling and marine experiences
  • Optional Wasini Island coastal excursion
  • Flexible leisure time and tailor-made activities
  • Choice of accommodation categories

Your Coastal Journey

Detailed 4-Day Itinerary

This is a suggested programme that can be adjusted according to your arrival arrangements, selected hotel and preferred activities.

Day 01 · Arrival in Diani

Arrival, Hotel Check-in and Beach Relaxation

Arrive in Diani Beach, where you will be met by your representative at the agreed pickup point. Depending on your arrival arrangements, transfer to your selected beach hotel or resort.

Complete the check-in process and settle into your accommodation. Take some time to relax after your journey and enjoy the tropical surroundings.

Later, enjoy a leisurely walk along the beautiful white sandy beach. Experience the warm Indian Ocean breeze, palm-lined coastline and peaceful atmosphere of the Kenyan coast.

Return to your hotel for dinner and overnight accommodation, according to your selected meal plan.

Overnight

Selected Diani Beach hotel or resort.

Arrival transfer Hotel check-in Beach relaxation Overnight stay
Day 02 · Beach and Ocean Experiences

A Day of Coastal Leisure and Optional Water Activities

Begin your day with breakfast at your hotel before enjoying a relaxed morning by the beach.

Spend time swimming where conditions are safe, relaxing by the shoreline or enjoying the resort's facilities.

For travellers interested in additional experiences, optional activities may include snorkelling, glass-bottom boat trips, diving or other marine excursions, subject to availability, weather and sea conditions.

Return to your hotel later in the day and enjoy the remainder of your afternoon at leisure. Dinner and overnight stay at your selected accommodation.

Overnight

Selected Diani Beach hotel or resort.

Breakfast Beach leisure Optional water activities Overnight stay
Day 03 · Coastal Discovery

Optional Coastal Excursion or a Free Leisure Day

After breakfast, enjoy another beautiful day along the Kenyan coast. You can choose to spend the day at leisure or add an optional coastal excursion to your holiday.

One popular option is a full-day Wasini Island and Kisite Mpunguti Marine Park excursion. This experience may include a boat trip, marine sightseeing, snorkelling where conditions allow and time to discover the local coastal environment.

Alternatively, remain in Diani and enjoy the beach, hotel facilities, local shopping or a relaxed afternoon exploring the surrounding area.

Return to your accommodation for your final evening in Diani.

Overnight

Selected Diani Beach hotel or resort.

Breakfast Optional coastal excursion Free leisure time Overnight stay
Day 04 · Departure

Breakfast, Check-out and Departure

Enjoy your final breakfast at the hotel and take in the beautiful coastal atmosphere before preparing for departure.

Complete check-out formalities and meet your driver for the agreed transfer to your next destination, airport, railway station or other agreed drop-off point.

Depending on your departure schedule, you may have some additional free time before leaving Diani.

Your coastal escape comes to an end with beautiful memories of Kenya's south coast.
