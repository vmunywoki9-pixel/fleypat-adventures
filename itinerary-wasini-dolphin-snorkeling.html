Discover the Kenyan Coast

A Day of Ocean Discovery and Island Culture

The Wasini Island excursion combines a boat journey, marine wildlife watching, a snorkeling opportunity and a visit to one of the Kenyan coast's distinctive coral islands.

The marine adventure takes place around Kisite-Mpunguti Marine Park and Reserve, reached by boat from the Shimoni area in Kwale County. The protected marine environment is known for coral reefs, tropical fish, sea turtles and dolphins.

After the marine activities, the excursion continues to Wasini Island. Visitors can enjoy a Swahili-style meal and, where included in the arrangements, explore the island's coral garden and community boardwalk.

This experience is suitable for visitors who want to combine coastal scenery, marine activities and local island culture in one day.

Your Day by the Ocean

Suggested Wasini Island Itinerary

The schedule below is indicative. The order and timing of activities may change depending on tides, sea conditions, marine park procedures and the boat operator's arrangements.

Morning · Mainland to the Marine Park

Departure and Boat Excursion

Your day begins with pickup from your agreed location in Mombasa or the Diani area, depending on the package booked. Travel south towards Shimoni, the mainland departure point for many Wasini and Kisite excursions.

On arrival, meet the local boat crew and receive instructions about the day's excursion, safety procedures and marine environment. Board the arranged boat and begin the journey towards Kisite-Mpunguti.

Enjoy the coastal scenery and open-water views as the boat travels through the marine area.

✓ Agreed pickup ✓ Road transfer to Shimoni ✓ Boat departure
Morning · Marine Wildlife Watching

Look Out for Wild Dolphins

During the boat excursion, keep watch for dolphins moving through the surrounding waters. The area is known for marine wildlife, and seeing dolphins in their natural environment can be a memorable part of the trip.

Your crew will guide the boat according to local marine-watching practices. Dolphins should be observed responsibly without chasing, touching, feeding or attempting to surround them.

Please remember that dolphins are wild animals. Their movements are unpredictable, and sightings are never guaranteed.

✓ Marine wildlife observation ✓ Responsible viewing ✓ Scenic boat cruise
Late Morning · Kisite-Mpunguti

Snorkeling and Coral Reef Exploration

If weather, tides, visibility and park conditions permit, enjoy a snorkeling session at the designated marine area. Discover the underwater environment and look for colourful reef fish and coral formations.

Snorkeling equipment and guidance should be confirmed with the operator before departure. Follow all safety instructions and use a flotation aid if recommended.

Visitors who are not comfortable swimming in open water should discuss suitable alternatives with the tour team before booking.

✓ Snorkeling opportunity ✓ Reef and fish observation ✓ Subject to sea conditions
Afternoon · Wasini Island

Island Visit and Coral Garden Boardwalk

Continue to Wasini Island for a land-based experience. The island is known for its coral formations, coastal vegetation, mangroves and traditional Swahili community.

Depending on the package and available time, visit the community-managed coral garden boardwalk. This provides an opportunity to learn about the island's fossil coral landscape and mangrove environment.

A local guide may explain the area's natural features and the role of community initiatives in welcoming visitors and supporting conservation.

✓ Wasini Island visit ✓ Coral garden experience ✓ Community-guided option
Lunch · Island Cuisine

Enjoy a Swahili-Style Coastal Meal

Sit down for a relaxed coastal lunch on Wasini Island. Depending on the restaurant and meal arrangement, the menu may feature seafood, fish, coconut rice, chapati and other Swahili-inspired dishes.

Vegetarian options and special dietary requirements should be communicated in advance so that suitable arrangements can be checked with the restaurant.

After lunch, begin the return boat journey to the mainland and continue to your agreed drop-off point in Mombasa or Diani.

✓ Island lunch ✓ Return boat transfer ✓ Agreed drop-off

Travel with Care

Responsible Marine Tourism

Help protect the marine environment and support responsible tourism practices throughout your visit.

Respect Marine Wildlife

Observe dolphins and other marine animals from a respectful distance. Never chase, touch or feed wild marine wildlife.

Protect the Coral Reef

Avoid standing on, touching or removing coral. Use care when snorkeling and follow your guide's instructions around the reef.

Support the Island Community

Respect local customs, ask before photographing residents and consider supporting community-run activities during your visit.
