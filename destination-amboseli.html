Welcome to Amboseli

A land of giants and unforgettable views

Located in southern Kenya near the Tanzania border, Amboseli National Park is one of the country's most recognisable safari destinations. It is especially famous for its large elephant herds and striking views of Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest mountain.

The park's varied habitats include open grasslands, seasonal lake beds, woodlands and permanent swamps. These landscapes support a variety of wildlife and make Amboseli an excellent destination for nature lovers and photographers.

Discover the park

Experiences that make Amboseli special

Explore the landscapes and wildlife that give Amboseli its distinctive character.

Elephant Encounters

Amboseli is well known for its elephant population. Game drives provide opportunities to observe family groups and large bulls moving through the park's varied habitats.

Swamps and Wetlands

Amboseli's wetlands are important water sources in an otherwise dry landscape. They attract wildlife and birds, creating rewarding opportunities for observation and photography.

Birdlife and Nature

Amboseli supports a variety of bird species, particularly around its wetland areas. Birdlife changes with water levels, season and habitat conditions.

The mountain backdrop

Experience Mount Kilimanjaro views

One of Amboseli's most memorable features is the view of Mount Kilimanjaro across the border in Tanzania. The mountain creates a dramatic backdrop to the park's open plains and wildlife.

Clear views are often best in the early morning, although visibility depends on weather and cloud cover. The mountain may be hidden at other times, so its appearance should never be guaranteed as part of a safari.

  • Scenic landscapes and mountain photography
  • Early-morning game drives
  • Open plains and acacia woodland
  • Memorable elephant photography opportunities
Life around the swamps

Discover Amboseli's wetland ecosystem

Although much of Amboseli is dry and open, underground water flowing from the Kilimanjaro region helps sustain important wetland areas. These swamps provide water and vegetation for elephants and other wildlife.

During a game drive, you may see animals gathering around these habitats, feeding or moving through the marshes. The landscape changes with rainfall and water levels, giving the park different character throughout the year.

Wetland areas are also valuable for birdwatching and understanding how wildlife adapts to a semi-arid environment.

A different perspective

Visit Observation Hill

Observation Hill is a well-known viewpoint in Amboseli National Park. From the raised vantage point, visitors can appreciate the scale of the surrounding plains, wetland areas and distant mountain scenery when visibility allows.

It is a rewarding stop for landscape photography and a chance to take in the park beyond the safari vehicle. Access and visitor arrangements are subject to current park regulations and guidance from your safari driver or guide.

People and heritage

Explore Maasai culture and traditions

The Amboseli region is home to Maasai communities whose pastoral traditions, knowledge of the land and cultural heritage are closely connected to the surrounding environment.

Depending on your itinerary, a community visit may offer an opportunity to learn about traditional homes, beadwork, music, dance and aspects of daily life. These visits should be arranged respectfully with local hosts and community representatives.

Support community-led experiences, follow your hosts' guidance and always ask permission before taking photographs of people.
Prepare for your journey

Planning your Amboseli safari

A well-planned visit helps you make the most of the park's landscapes and wildlife. Your travel dates, accommodation, starting point and preferred activities will help determine the best itinerary.

Best Time to Visit

Amboseli can be visited throughout the year. Dry periods may make some wildlife easier to spot around water sources, while wetter periods can bring greener scenery and different birdlife. Conditions vary annually.

Getting There

Road transfers from Nairobi are a common way to reach Amboseli. Flight options may also be available to the park's airstrip, depending on schedules and your accommodation arrangements.

Where to Stay

Accommodation options include safari lodges, tented camps and other properties in and around the park. Location, facilities, meal plans and park access should be considered when choosing.

How Long to Stay

A two- or three-night safari is a popular introduction. A longer stay allows more time for game drives, photography, relaxation and exploring different parts of the park.

What to Pack

Pack comfortable clothing, a light jacket for early mornings, comfortable walking shoes, a hat, sunscreen, binoculars and a camera. Your camp can advise on seasonal conditions.

Park Fees and Rules

Park fees, entry conditions and conservation charges can change. Confirm the applicable rates for your travel dates. Follow park rules, remain in authorised areas and respect wildlife viewing distances.
