The beauty of Tanzania

Discover Tanzania's extraordinary landscapes

Tanzania is one of East Africa's most celebrated travel destinations. Its northern safari circuit is renowned for vast wildlife habitats, dramatic scenery and some of the continent's most memorable game-viewing experiences.

Beyond the mainland, the Indian Ocean islands offer a different pace of travel, with historic Stone Town, beautiful beaches, marine experiences and relaxing coastal retreats.

Explore Tanzania

Experiences to make your journey memorable

Choose from iconic wildlife destinations, dramatic natural scenery and beautiful Indian Ocean escapes. Your itinerary can focus on one region or combine several experiences.

Wildlife & wilderness

Serengeti National Park

Explore wide-open savannahs and rich wildlife habitats. The Serengeti is famous for its big cats and the annual wildebeest migration, whose timing and location vary with the seasons.

Natural wonder

Ngorongoro Crater

Discover the spectacular volcanic caldera, its surrounding highlands and a remarkable ecosystem supporting a variety of wildlife. Game viewing depends on conditions and animal movement.

Safari moments

Wildlife Encounters

Experience guided game drives and the excitement of observing wildlife in its natural environment. Sightings are never guaranteed, making every safari unique.

Scenic landscapes

Highlands & Crater Views

Enjoy the cooler highland environment, sweeping views and changing landscapes around the Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

Island escape

Zanzibar Beaches

Relax along the Indian Ocean coastline, enjoy beachside stays and explore the island's coastal atmosphere. Nungwi is one of Zanzibar's well-known northern beach destinations.

Ocean & relaxation

Indian Ocean Experiences

Include time for swimming, boat excursions, snorkelling or simply enjoying the coastline. Activities should be selected according to weather, sea conditions and local availability.

Northern safari circuit

Experience Tanzania's famous safari country

A northern Tanzania safari can bring together several distinctive destinations. The Serengeti offers vast plains and renowned wildlife viewing, while Ngorongoro provides a dramatic crater environment.

Other destinations can add a different dimension to the journey:

  • Tarangire National Park: known for its elephant populations, baobab trees and seasonal wildlife concentrations.
  • Lake Manyara National Park: a varied landscape of woodland, lake shore and wildlife habitats.
  • Mount Kilimanjaro: an iconic mountain destination for trekking and scenic adventures.

Beyond the safari

Discover Zanzibar's island atmosphere

Zanzibar offers a wonderful contrast to a mainland safari. Explore the historic streets of Stone Town, learn about the island's spice heritage, discover coastal villages or spend time beside the Indian Ocean.

Beach destinations such as Nungwi and other coastal areas offer opportunities for relaxation and selected water activities. Excursions and marine experiences can be arranged according to local conditions and availability.
