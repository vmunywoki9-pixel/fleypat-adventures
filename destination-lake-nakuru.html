Discover the Beauty of Lake Nakuru

Set within Kenya's Great Rift Valley, Lake Nakuru National Park offers an exceptional combination of lake scenery, wooded hills, open grassland and wildlife habitats.

The park is particularly well known for its white and black rhinos, diverse birdlife and opportunities to encounter some of Kenya's most recognisable wildlife. Its landscapes change with the seasons, creating a rewarding setting for photography and guided game drives.

Whether you are planning a day trip or a longer safari, Lake Nakuru can be explored on its own or included in a wider Kenya itinerary.

What to Experience in Lake Nakuru

From memorable wildlife encounters to lakeside scenery, the park offers a variety of experiences for nature lovers and safari travellers.

01 · WILDLIFE

Rhino Encounters

Lake Nakuru is an important sanctuary for both black and white rhinos. A guided game drive offers opportunities to look for these impressive animals in their natural habitat.

02 · BIRDLIFE

Flamingos and Waterbirds

The lake and surrounding wetlands attract a variety of waterbirds. Flamingo numbers and their distribution vary with changing water and food conditions.

03 · NATURE

Wildlife and Wetlands

Look out for buffaloes, waterbirds and other wildlife around the lake and its surrounding habitats during a relaxed game drive.

Views and landscapes

Take in the Views from Baboon Cliff

Lake Nakuru's viewpoints reveal the scale and beauty of the park. Baboon Cliff is known for its elevated views across the lake, surrounding vegetation and distant hills.

It is a rewarding stop for landscape photography and a chance to appreciate the wider setting of this Rift Valley destination.

Beyond the lakeshore

More Places to Explore

Lake Nakuru National Park has a variety of landscapes and natural features that make a visit more than just a lakeside experience.

Makalia Falls

A scenic waterfall in the southern part of the park, offering a different perspective on the park's natural environment. Water flow and access conditions can vary.

Acacia Woodlands

Explore areas of woodland and open vegetation that provide habitats for different bird and mammal species.

Rhino Habitat

The park is recognised for its rhino conservation importance. Sightings depend on animal movements and the conditions on the day.

Lake and Rift Valley Scenery

Enjoy changing views across the lake, the surrounding hills and the varied landscapes that define this part of the Great Rift Valley.

Before you travel

Planning Your Lake Nakuru Safari

We help you organise the practical details so your visit fits your travel plans and preferred safari experience.

Getting There

Lake Nakuru National Park is located near Nakuru town in Kenya's Rift Valley. Your trip can be arranged from Nakuru or as part of a longer journey from Nairobi, subject to your itinerary.

Best Time to Visit

The park can be visited throughout the year. Weather, road conditions, lake levels and wildlife movements influence the experience, so plans should remain flexible.

What to Bring

Consider comfortable clothing, a hat, sunscreen, drinking water, binoculars and a camera. A light jacket may be useful during cooler mornings.

Safari Duration

A day visit is possible, while an overnight stay gives you more time to explore and can be combined with other Rift Valley destinations.

Accommodation

Accommodation options can be discussed according to your preferred comfort level, budget, location and availability for your travel dates.

Park Entry and Fees

Applicable entry fees, access requirements and operating arrangements should be checked and confirmed for your travel date before departure.

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. Flamingo numbers and distribution can change with lake conditions. Activities, park access, fees and accommodation are subject to confirmation at the time of booking.
