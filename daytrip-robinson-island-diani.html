A Unique Coastal Experience

Discover the Robinson Island Sandbank

Just offshore from Diani Beach, the place commonly known as Robinson Island is a tidal sandbank surrounded by shallow Indian Ocean waters. It becomes exposed around low tide and is covered again as the water rises.

The excursion combines a scenic boat ride with the opportunity to view the underwater environment through a glass-bottom boat. Depending on the conditions and package selected, guests can also enjoy swimming, snorkelling and time on the sandbank.

  • Glass-bottom boat excursion from Diani
  • Views of the clear coastal waters and reef environment
  • Time on the exposed sandbank at low tide
  • Optional swimming and snorkelling
  • Beautiful coastal photography opportunities

Your Robinson Island Experience

Detailed Half-Day Itinerary

The programme is arranged around the tide. Your exact departure time will be confirmed after checking the tidal conditions for your chosen date.

Stage 01 · Departure

Meet Your Boat Crew

Begin at the agreed departure point along Diani Beach. Meet your local boat crew, confirm the excursion arrangements and receive a safety briefing before boarding.

  • Hotel pickup if included in your booking
  • Transfer to the agreed beach departure point
  • Boat safety briefing and boarding
Stage 02 · Ocean Cruise

Glass-Bottom Boat Experience

Set out across the Indian Ocean in a glass-bottom boat. Enjoy views of the coastal waters and, where visibility and the route allow, observe fish and reef features through the viewing panels.

Your crew will guide the excursion and explain the marine environment along the way.

Stage 03 · Marine Discovery

Optional Snorkelling & Reef Viewing

Depending on the operator's route and sea conditions, the boat may stop at a suitable reef area for snorkelling. Guests who prefer to remain on the boat can continue enjoying the views through the glass panels.

  • Optional guided snorkelling
  • Opportunity to observe reef fish and marine habitats
  • Follow the crew's instructions throughout
  • Snorkelling equipment confirmed with your package
Stage 04 · Sandbank Time

Relax at Robinson Island

Arrive at the sandbank when the tide has exposed enough of the area for a suitable visit. Enjoy the shallow pools, take photographs and relax on the white sand.

  • Time on the sandbank when exposed
  • Swimming in suitable shallow-water areas
  • Photography and relaxation

Your crew will advise when it is time to return to the boat as the tide changes.

Stage 05 · Return Journey

Boat Ride Back to Diani Beach

After your sandbank visit, board the boat for the return journey to the mainland. Disembark at the agreed landing point and continue with your hotel transfer if this was included in your booking.

Your half-day Robinson Island experience ends with the option to continue enjoying the rest of your day in Diani.

Tide and itinerary note: Robinson Island in Diani is a tidal sandbank, not a permanent island. It becomes exposed around low tide and may be partly or fully covered at other times. The boat departure and time spent there must be arranged around the tide table and sea conditions.
Prepare for Your Excursion

Tour Information

A few useful details to help you enjoy your Robinson Island sandbank experience.

What to Bring

  • Swimwear and a change of clothes
  • Towel and reef-friendly footwear
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat
  • Waterproof bag for your phone and valuables
  • Drinking water
  • Camera or smartphone

Marine Safety & Conservation

  • Follow the boat crew's safety briefing
  • Wear a life jacket when instructed
  • Stay close to your guide during water activities
  • Do not stand on or damage living coral
  • Do not collect shells or marine animals
  • Keep children under close adult supervision

Tide-Dependent Departure

The departure time changes according to the tide. Your confirmed time will be shared after checking conditions for your travel date.

Swimming & Snorkelling

Both are optional activities. Non-swimmers can remain on the boat and enjoy the glass-bottom viewing experience.

Pickup & Transfers

Hotel transfers are subject to the package selected. Confirm your hotel location and pickup arrangements when booking.
