About This Holiday

This seven-day private holiday brings together Zanzibar's cultural heritage, tropical nature and beautiful coastline. Begin in historic Stone Town, where narrow streets, historic buildings and lively markets tell the story of the island's diverse influences.

Explore Prison Island and its giant Aldabra tortoises, discover the island's spice-growing traditions, and visit Jozani Forest to look for the rare Zanzibar red colobus monkey.

Continue to Kendwa Beach for a relaxing coastal stay, with opportunities for snorkeling around Mnemba, responsible dolphin viewing and the full-day Safari Blue experience.

Your island journey

Detailed 7-Day Itinerary

Enjoy a combination of culture, nature, marine adventures and beach relaxation across seven days in Zanzibar.

Day 01

Arrival in Zanzibar – Transfer to Stone Town

Upon arrival at Abeid Amani Karume International Airport, you will be welcomed by your local representative and transferred to your selected accommodation in Stone Town.

After check-in, take time to settle in and enjoy the atmosphere of Zanzibar's historic centre. Depending on your arrival time, you may explore the nearby streets independently or visit Forodhani Gardens in the evening.

Overnight in Stone Town.

Destination: Stone Town Activity: Arrival & Leisure Meal Plan: Breakfast
Day 02

Stone Town Heritage Tour & Prison Island

After breakfast, meet your local guide for a walking tour of Stone Town. Explore its narrow streets and discover important landmarks that reflect the island's rich history.

Depending on access and the tour route, visit Freddie Mercury House, the Old Fort, the Sultan's Palace area, the former Slave Market and local markets.

Later, take a boat excursion to Prison Island. Explore the island and see its famous giant Aldabra tortoises. Enjoy time around the island before returning to Stone Town.

Return to your hotel for the evening.

Activities: Stone Town & Prison Island Accommodation: Stone Town Meal Plan: Breakfast
Day 03

Spice Farm, Jozani Forest & Transfer to Kendwa

After breakfast, check out and begin your island exploration with a visit to a traditional spice farm. Discover how Zanzibar's famous spices are grown and used, including cloves, cinnamon, vanilla and nutmeg.

Enjoy the opportunity to see and learn about tropical fruits and aromatic plants during the guided experience.

Continue to Jozani Forest, the best-known forest reserve on the island. Explore its forest trails and look out for the Zanzibar red colobus monkey. Continue along the mangrove boardwalk to learn about this coastal ecosystem.

Later, transfer to Kendwa on the northern coast and check in at your selected beach accommodation.

Activities: Spice Farm & Jozani Route: Stone Town – Kendwa Accommodation: Kendwa
Day 04

Leisure Day at Kendwa Beach

Enjoy a relaxed day at Kendwa Beach. After breakfast, spend time on the white-sand shoreline, enjoy the ocean views or relax at your hotel.

Kendwa is a popular destination for swimming and beach relaxation. You may also choose to arrange optional experiences such as a sunset dhow cruise, additional snorkeling or a spa treatment.

Optional activities are not included unless specifically stated in your confirmed quotation.

Overnight in Kendwa.

Destination: Kendwa Beach Activity: Leisure Accommodation: Kendwa
Day 05

Mnemba Snorkeling & Dolphin-Viewing Excursion

After breakfast, depart for your scheduled marine excursion around the Mnemba area. Enjoy a boat trip to the marine environment around Mnemba Island, where conditions may provide opportunities for snorkeling.

Explore the underwater world with your snorkeling equipment and look out for colourful marine life. The excursion may also include opportunities to observe dolphins in their natural environment.

Dolphin sightings are not guaranteed. Viewing should follow responsible wildlife practices, with no chasing, crowding or disturbing the animals.

Enjoy the included lunch during the excursion before returning to Kendwa.

Activity: Mnemba Marine Excursion Snorkeling Equipment Included Lunch Included Accommodation: Kendwa
Day 06

Safari Blue – Dhow Cruise & Marine Adventure

After breakfast, transfer to Fumba for the Safari Blue experience in the Menai Bay area. Board a traditional dhow and set out to explore the beautiful coastal waters.

Depending on tides and sea conditions, enjoy opportunities for snorkeling, visiting sandbanks and exploring selected islands or coastal locations.

A seafood BBQ lunch is served as part of the experience. Enjoy the island atmosphere and time on the water before returning to the departure point.

Transfer back to your Kendwa accommodation for your final night in Zanzibar.

Activity: Safari Blue Traditional Dhow Excursion Seafood BBQ Lunch Included Accommodation: Kendwa
Day 07

Departure from Zanzibar

Enjoy breakfast at your hotel before checking out. At the scheduled time, meet your driver for a private transfer to Abeid Amani Karume International Airport.

Arrive at the airport in good time for your departure arrangements and onward flight.

Your Zanzibar holiday comes to an end, leaving you with memories of the island's culture, beaches and marine experiences.

Activity: Airport Transfer Meal Plan: Breakfast End of Package

Prepare for your island holiday

Practical Information

  • Passport and entry requirements: Ensure that your passport is valid and check the entry requirements applicable to your nationality before travelling.
  • Zanzibar inbound travel insurance: Visitors are subject to Zanzibar's mandatory inbound travel insurance requirements. The applicable policy and arrangements will be confirmed before travel.
  • Flights: International and domestic flights are not included in the stated package price. Flight options can be quoted according to your departure country and preferred travel dates.
  • Clothing: Pack light tropical clothing, swimwear, comfortable walking shoes, sun protection and suitable clothing for cultural visits. Dress respectfully when visiting religious and local community sites.
  • Marine activities: Snorkeling and boat excursions depend on weather, tides and sea conditions. Operators may adjust schedules when necessary.
  • Dolphin viewing: Dolphins are wild animals. Sightings are not guaranteed and responsible viewing practices should always be followed.
  • Accommodation: Hotels are subject to availability. Your chosen properties and room arrangements will be confirmed before payment.
  • Itinerary flexibility: The sequence of activities may change due to weather, operational arrangements or marine conditions while maintaining the main experiences wherever possible.
  • Meals: Daily breakfast is included. Lunches are included during the specified Mnemba and Safari Blue excursions. Other meals are at your own expense unless otherwise stated.
