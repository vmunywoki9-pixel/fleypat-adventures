Discover the South Coast

A Day of Ocean Discovery

The Wasini Dolphin Tour is one of the memorable marine experiences available along Kenya's southern coastline. Departing from the Shimoni area, the excursion takes visitors into the waters around the Kisite-Mpunguti marine environment.

The experience may include dolphin observation, snorkelling at suitable reef locations and time on Wasini Island. It is an opportunity to appreciate the Indian Ocean, marine biodiversity and the coastal communities of the South Coast.

Your Marine Adventure

Suggested Full-Day Itinerary

The schedule below is indicative. Actual departure and activity times depend on your pickup location, tides, weather, sea conditions, marine park arrangements and the local boat operator.

Early Morning · Approximately 6:00–7:30 AM

Pickup & Transfer to Shimoni

Your day begins with pickup from your hotel or agreed location along the South Coast. Guests travelling from Diani, Mombasa or other areas should confirm the transfer arrangements and departure time in advance.

Travel towards Shimoni, the coastal departure area for the boat excursion. On arrival, meet the local operator and receive information about the day's marine activities and safety procedures.

  • Pickup from the agreed coastal location
  • Transfer to Shimoni
  • Meet the boat operator and crew
  • Receive the marine excursion briefing
  • Confirm the day's sea and weather conditions
Morning · Subject to Sea Conditions

Boat Excursion into the Marine Area

Board the boat and travel into the marine waters around Kisite-Mpunguti. Enjoy views of the Indian Ocean and the coastal landscape as the excursion moves towards suitable marine viewing areas.

The crew will guide the route according to weather, sea conditions, park requirements and the day's operating arrangements.

  • Board the arranged boat
  • Travel through the coastal marine environment
  • Enjoy scenic ocean views
  • Listen to information from the local crew
  • Follow all onboard safety instructions
Morning · Wildlife Observation

Dolphin Watching in Their Natural Habitat

One of the main attractions of this excursion is the opportunity to observe dolphins in the wild. The boat crew may help locate areas where dolphins are known to travel.

Dolphins are wild animals and their movements cannot be predicted. The tour does not guarantee an encounter, swimming with dolphins or close contact.

  • Look for dolphins from the boat
  • Observe their natural behaviour from a respectful distance
  • Enjoy wildlife photography where conditions allow
  • Avoid chasing, touching or feeding marine wildlife
  • Follow the crew's responsible wildlife-viewing guidance
Late Morning · Conditions Permitting

Snorkelling & Marine Exploration

Where sea conditions and visibility are suitable, enjoy a snorkelling session at an authorised location within the marine area.

Discover the underwater environment, which may include colourful reef fish and coral formations. Snorkelling arrangements depend on safety conditions, tide, visibility and the operator's equipment.

  • Receive snorkelling instructions
  • Use the appropriate safety equipment
  • Explore the reef only at permitted locations
  • Do not stand on, touch or remove coral
  • Follow the guide's instructions throughout the activity

Guests who do not wish to snorkel can remain on the boat or follow the alternative arrangements offered by the operator.

Afternoon · Subject to Tour Arrangements

Wasini Island Visit & Lunch

Continue to Wasini Island for the island portion of your excursion. Depending on your selected package, enjoy a local meal and an opportunity to experience the island's coastal setting.

The visit may include time to relax, learn about the local community and enjoy the surrounding scenery. Specific activities and lunch arrangements will be confirmed before the trip.

  • Arrive at the island according to the boat schedule
  • Enjoy lunch if included in your package
  • Experience the local island atmosphere
  • Explore agreed visitor areas
  • Respect local customs and community guidelines
Afternoon · Return to Shimoni

Return Boat Transfer & Drop-Off

After completing the island and marine activities, return by boat to Shimoni. Meet your driver and begin the transfer back to your agreed coastal destination.

Arrival time will depend on the boat schedule, weather, road traffic and the location of your accommodation.

  • Return boat transfer to Shimoni
  • Meet your driver for the return journey
  • Transfer to the agreed hotel or drop-off location
  • End of your Wasini marine experience
Responsible Ocean Experiences

Enjoy the Marine Environment Responsibly

The ocean is home to delicate ecosystems and wild animals. Responsible tourism helps protect the experience for future visitors and local communities.

Respect Wild Dolphins

Dolphins should be observed without chasing, touching, feeding or attempting to force an interaction. Their natural behaviour and freedom should always come first.

Protect Coral Reefs

Avoid touching or standing on coral, collecting marine life or leaving rubbish in the water. Use reef-conscious practices during snorkelling.

Follow Safety Guidance

Listen to the boat crew, wear recommended safety equipment and follow instructions about swimming, snorkelling and movement around the vessel.

Support Local Communities

Respect local customs, follow visitor guidance and support responsible local services and businesses where appropriate.

Before You Travel

Important Marine Tour Information

Starting Point

The boat excursion normally departs from the Shimoni area. Road transfers from Diani, Mombasa and other coastal locations should be confirmed when booking.

Sea & Weather Conditions

Boat routes, snorkelling and other marine activities depend on sea conditions, tides, weather, visibility and safety assessments made by the local operator.

What to Bring

  • Swimwear and a change of clothes
  • Towel and comfortable sandals
  • Sun protection and sunglasses
  • Waterproof bag for personal belongings
  • Camera or waterproof phone protection
  • Any personal medication you may need

Swimming & Snorkelling

Swimming and snorkelling are subject to conditions, safety arrangements and individual ability. Inform the operator if you are not comfortable in the water.

Dolphin Sightings

Dolphins are wild animals. Their presence cannot be guaranteed, and the excursion should not involve chasing, touching, feeding or forced interaction.

Marine Park Regulations

Follow marine park rules and crew instructions. Do not collect coral, shells or marine organisms from protected areas, and avoid damaging reef habitats.

Important booking note

Your final quotation will confirm pickup and drop-off arrangements, boat operator, marine park fees, snorkelling equipment, lunch, applicable taxes, payment arrangements and cancellation terms. The exact activity sequence may change due to tides, sea conditions, weather and local operating requirements. Dolphin sightings and snorkelling conditions cannot be guaranteed.
