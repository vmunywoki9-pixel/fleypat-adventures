Discover the Southern Coast

A Marine Adventure Around Wasini

The Wasini and Kisite-Mpunguti area is known for its clear coastal waters, coral reef environment and boat excursions departing from Shimoni.

This full-day experience combines a journey on the Indian Ocean with time to appreciate the marine scenery. Depending on the package and conditions, the outing may include dolphin watching, a visit to the Kisite Marine Park area, snorkelling and time connected with Wasini Island.

Responsible wildlife viewing: Dolphins are wild animals. Sightings cannot be guaranteed, and boats should maintain respectful distances without chasing, surrounding or disturbing them.

Your Day-by-Day Experience

Full-Day Wasini Tour Itinerary

The sequence and timing of activities may change according to tides, sea conditions, park regulations and the arrangements confirmed by your local operator.

Morning · Arrival at Shimoni

Transfer to Shimoni and Boat Briefing

Travel to Shimoni from your agreed pickup location. On arrival, meet the local boat team and receive a briefing about the day's route, safety procedures and marine park guidelines.

  • Pickup from the agreed hotel or meeting point.
  • Road transfer to Shimoni jetty.
  • Meet the boat crew and prepare for departure.
Morning · Boat excursion

Dolphin Watching Experience

Head out onto the Indian Ocean and watch for dolphins in the waters around the marine park. Your boat crew will follow responsible wildlife-viewing practices and local guidance.

Dolphin sightings depend on natural movement and conditions at sea. The tour continues with the planned marine experience even when dolphins are not encountered.

Late morning · Marine activity

Snorkelling and Marine Exploration

Where included in your package and permitted by the day's conditions, enjoy time in the water to observe the reef environment and marine life.

Snorkelling is subject to sea conditions, visibility, tides, safety instructions and the applicable park rules. Guests who prefer not to snorkel can enjoy the boat and ocean scenery.

Afternoon · Return to shore

Coastal Return and End of Tour

After the marine excursion, return towards Shimoni according to the boat operator's schedule. If your selected package includes a Wasini Island visit or meal, these arrangements will be confirmed before travel.

Meet your driver for the return transfer to your hotel or agreed drop-off point, bringing your coastal day trip to an end.

Before You Travel

Useful Tour Information

What to Bring

  • Swimwear and a towel if you plan to snorkel.
  • Reef-friendly sun protection where appropriate.
  • Hat, sunglasses and drinking water.
  • Waterproof bag for personal belongings.
  • Comfortable clothing and sandals.
  • Personal medication and any required travel essentials.

Good to Know

  • Boat schedules depend on tides and sea conditions.
  • Dolphin sightings are not guaranteed.
  • Snorkelling depends on safety and visibility conditions.
  • Follow the boat crew's instructions at all times.
  • Do not feed, touch or chase marine wildlife.
  • Confirm park fees, meals and transfers before payment.
