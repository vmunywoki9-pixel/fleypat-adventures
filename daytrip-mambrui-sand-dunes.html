Discover Mambrui

Kenya's Beautiful Coastal Sand Dunes

Located near Malindi in Kilifi County, Mambrui is known for its unusual landscape of rolling coastal sand dunes beside the Indian Ocean.

Often called Dubai Ndogo, the dunes offer a distinctive experience along Kenya's northern coast. Their changing shapes, soft sandy ridges and coastal setting create a beautiful environment for exploration and photography.

The tour gives you time to appreciate the scenery, walk through selected areas and enjoy the coastal atmosphere. Optional activities may also be arranged through local operators.

Travel responsibly: Mambrui's dunes are a natural coastal environment. Follow local guide instructions, respect marked areas and avoid damaging vegetation or disturbing sensitive dune sections.

Your Day Experience

Mambrui Sand Dunes Itinerary

The following is a suggested full-day programme. The exact schedule can be adjusted depending on your pickup location, selected activities and local operating arrangements.

Morning · Departure

Hotel Pickup and Journey to Mambrui

Begin your day with pickup from your agreed hotel or meeting point in Malindi, Watamu or another confirmed coastal location.

Travel towards Mambrui, passing through the coastal surroundings and nearby settlements before reaching the sand dune area.

  • Pickup from your agreed location.
  • Scenic road transfer towards Mambrui.
  • Arrival and orientation with the local guide or operator.
Morning · Main attraction

Explore the Mambrui Sand Dunes

Spend time exploring the dunes with a local guide. Walk along suitable sections, enjoy the changing sandy formations and take in the open coastal views.

Your guide can help identify suitable photography locations and explain the natural landscape and the importance of respecting the surrounding environment.

Midday · Optional activities

Adventure and Local Experiences

If you wish to add more adventure to your visit, ask about activities offered by local operators. Depending on availability, these may include quad biking, horse riding or other experiences.

Activities are optional and may require separate payment. Confirm the operator, price, duration, safety equipment and applicable restrictions before participating.

Afternoon · Relaxation and return

Final Photography and Return Journey

Enjoy some final time appreciating the landscape and taking photographs before leaving the dune area. Lunch or refreshments can be arranged according to your selected package.

After the visit, meet your driver for the return transfer to your hotel or agreed drop-off point, bringing your Mambrui day tour to an end.

Before You Travel

Useful Tour Information

What to Bring

  • Comfortable walking shoes or sandals.
  • Light and comfortable clothing.
  • Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen.
  • Drinking water.
  • Camera or phone for photography.
  • Some cash for optional local activities.

Good to Know

  • Walking on soft sand can be physically demanding.
  • Temperatures and wind conditions can vary.
  • Optional activities depend on local availability.
  • Follow local guide instructions around the dunes.
  • Confirm all charges and activity arrangements before travel.
  • Do not assume swimming is safe at the nearby shoreline.
