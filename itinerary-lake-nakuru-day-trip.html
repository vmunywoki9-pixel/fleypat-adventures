Discover Lake Nakuru

A Day Among the Wild Beauty of the Rift Valley

Lake Nakuru National Park is one of Kenya's celebrated wildlife destinations, offering a beautiful combination of lake scenery, woodland, grassland and wildlife habitats.

This day trip is designed for travellers who want to experience Kenya's wildlife without committing to a multi-day safari. Explore the park with a game drive, enjoy the scenery and look out for some of its well-known wildlife.

The park is particularly recognised for its rhino conservation, Rothschild's giraffes and birdlife. Depending on the season and conditions, visitors may also encounter buffalo, zebras, waterbucks, baboons and other animals.

Your Day in the Wild

Suggested Day Trip Itinerary

The schedule below is a suggested outline. Your final departure time and arrangements will depend on your pickup location, travel dates and confirmed booking details.

Morning · Departure and Arrival

Journey to Lake Nakuru National Park

Begin your day with pickup from your agreed location in Nairobi or Nakuru. If travelling from Nairobi, enjoy the journey through the Great Rift Valley, with an opportunity to appreciate the surrounding landscapes along the way.

On arrival, proceed with the park entry arrangements and begin your game drive. Your guide will help you explore different habitats while looking out for wildlife.

Midday · Wildlife Exploration

Explore the Park and Enjoy a Lunch Break

Continue exploring the park's varied landscapes as you search for wildlife. Lake Nakuru is known for its rhino population and diverse birdlife, while its woodlands and open areas provide habitats for several other species.

Take a break for a picnic or pre-arranged lunch at an appropriate designated location, subject to park rules and the arrangements confirmed in your quotation.

Afternoon · Return Journey

Final Game Viewing and Departure

Depending on the time available, enjoy additional game viewing before leaving the park. Take in the last views of the landscape and wildlife before beginning your return journey.

You will be transferred back to your agreed drop-off point in Nairobi or Nakuru, bringing your Lake Nakuru day trip to an end.

Wildlife Encounters

What You May See

Lake Nakuru supports a variety of wildlife and bird species. Sightings depend on the season, animal movements and conditions during your visit.

Rhinos

Lake Nakuru is well known for its rhino conservation and offers an opportunity to look for these remarkable animals.

Birdlife

The lake and surrounding habitats attract a wide variety of birds, with flamingo numbers varying according to changing lake conditions.

Rothschild's Giraffes

Look out for the distinctive Rothschild's giraffe, one of the special wildlife attractions associated with the park.
