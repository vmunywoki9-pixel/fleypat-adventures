School trip preparation

Important planning considerations

Good preparation helps schools organise a more comfortable and structured excursion.

Number of Learners

Provide the expected number of learners and accompanying teachers or supervisors.

Age Groups

Share the learners' age range or class levels so proposed experiences can be considered appropriately.

Learning Objectives

Explain what learners should discover or understand during the excursion.

Travel Dates

Provide preferred dates and flexibility in case alternative arrangements need to be considered.

Transport Requirements

Share your departure point, destination and group transport requirements.

Meals & Accommodation

Indicate whether meals, overnight accommodation or a day excursion are required.

Supervision Arrangements

Schools should establish suitable teacher and adult supervision arrangements for the group.

Safety & Accessibility

Share relevant accessibility needs and any special considerations when requesting your proposal.

Frequently asked questions

School & Educational Tours FAQs

Useful information for teachers, school administrators and group coordinators.

Can you organise educational trips for primary and secondary schools?

We can discuss proposed excursions for different learner age groups and school levels. The final itinerary should be appropriate for the learners and approved by the institution.

Can we request a customised educational itinerary?

Yes. Share your learning objectives, preferred destination, group size and travel dates so we can prepare a proposed itinerary for consideration.

Do you arrange transport for school groups?

We can discuss group transport requirements as part of the travel proposal, subject to vehicle and supplier availability and confirmation.

Can a school organise a one-day excursion?

Yes. You can enquire about day excursions, including wildlife, nature, cultural and other educational experiences.

Can you arrange overnight educational trips?

We can discuss multi-day excursions with accommodation and transport arrangements based on the school's requirements and available options.

Who is responsible for learner supervision?

The school or organising institution remains responsible for learner supervision, permissions and safeguarding arrangements. These should be agreed before the trip.

Planning and safety note: All school excursions should be planned in consultation with the institution's administration and relevant guardians. Schools should arrange appropriate supervision, permissions, emergency contacts, safeguarding measures and any required insurance. Activities and transport arrangements are subject to confirmation.

Ready to plan an educational adventure?

Tell us about your school, group size, learning objectives and preferred destination. We will help you explore suitable educational travel arrangements.
