Mombasa SGR Terminus

Continue your journey with a planned transfer

The Madaraka Express connects Mombasa and Nairobi through Kenya's Standard Gauge Railway. Mombasa Terminus is located at Miritini, outside Mombasa Island.

After your train journey, you may need onward transport to your accommodation, a meeting point, a beach destination or another location. Fleypat Adventures can help you arrange a transfer according to your travel plans.

How to arrange your SGR transfer

A few travel details will help us understand your requirements and prepare an appropriate quotation.

What to send us

Your train travel date, expected arrival or departure time, number of passengers, luggage requirements, pickup point and final destination.
