Discover Amboseli

An Unforgettable African Wilderness Experience

Amboseli National Park is one of Kenya's most celebrated wildlife destinations. Located at the foot of Mount Kilimanjaro, the park is famous for its large elephant populations, expansive plains and beautiful wetlands.

This three-day safari offers an opportunity to explore the park through guided game drives, observe wildlife in its natural habitat and enjoy the changing scenery of the African wilderness.

The journey begins in Nairobi, with road transfer to Amboseli and two nights at accommodation selected according to your budget and preferences.

  • Explore Amboseli National Park
  • Look out for large elephant herds
  • Enjoy views of Mount Kilimanjaro when weather permits
  • Experience morning and afternoon game drives
  • Choose accommodation to suit your budget

Your Safari Journey

Detailed 3-Day Itinerary

The following is a suggested itinerary. Actual departure times, game-drive schedules and meal arrangements will be confirmed when your safari is booked.

Day 01 · Nairobi to Amboseli

Journey to Amboseli National Park

Your safari begins with an early morning departure from Nairobi. Meet your driver-guide at the agreed pickup location before travelling south towards Amboseli National Park.

Enjoy the changing scenery along the route, passing through Kenya's countryside and local settlements. On arrival in the Amboseli area, proceed to your selected accommodation for check-in and lunch, subject to the accommodation's schedule.

In the afternoon, head out for your first game drive. Explore the park's open plains and swamp areas, which attract a variety of wildlife. Keep your camera ready for elephants, zebras, giraffes, buffaloes and other animals.

Return to the lodge or camp in the evening for dinner and overnight stay.

Overnight

Selected Amboseli lodge or tented camp.

Road Transfer Afternoon Game Drive Dinner Overnight Stay
Day 02 · Full Day in Amboseli

Wildlife Exploration and Scenic Game Drives

Wake up early for breakfast before setting out for a morning game drive. Early hours offer a wonderful opportunity to observe wildlife as animals become active across the park.

Explore different parts of Amboseli, including its open plains and marshland habitats. These areas support elephants and many other species, with the possibility of spotting predators such as lions and hyenas.

Return to your accommodation for lunch and some time to relax. Depending on your accommodation and the day's arrangements, you may enjoy the surrounding scenery before the afternoon activity.

In the afternoon, enjoy another game drive. Look out for elephants gathering around water sources and enjoy the changing colours of the landscape as the sun begins to set.

Return to the lodge or camp for dinner and your second night in Amboseli.

Overnight

Selected Amboseli lodge or tented camp.

Morning Game Drive Lunch Afternoon Game Drive Dinner
Day 03 · Amboseli to Nairobi

Final Game Drive and Return to Nairobi

Enjoy breakfast at the lodge or camp. Depending on your departure schedule, you may have an opportunity for a final morning game drive or a relaxed start to the day.

Take in the beautiful surroundings of Amboseli and enjoy any final wildlife sightings before checking out.

Begin the return journey to Nairobi, with an opportunity to enjoy the Kenyan countryside along the way. On arrival, you will be dropped off at your agreed location.

Your three-day Amboseli safari comes to an end with memorable experiences of Kenya's wildlife and landscapes.

Before You Travel

Important Safari Information

A few helpful details to prepare for your Amboseli safari experience.

Departure and Pickup

The safari starts in Nairobi. Your pickup location and departure time will be agreed before travel.

What to Pack

Bring comfortable clothing, closed shoes, a hat, sunscreen, sunglasses, personal medication and a camera or binoculars.

Best Wildlife Viewing

Early morning and late afternoon are popular times for wildlife viewing. Animal sightings are never guaranteed.

Weather and Kilimanjaro Views

Weather conditions can change. Clear views of Mount Kilimanjaro are not guaranteed and depend on visibility.
