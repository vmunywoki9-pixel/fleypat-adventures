A Journey Through Coastal History

Experience the many stories of Mombasa

Mombasa is a city shaped by centuries of trade and cultural exchange. Its historic streets reflect Swahili, Arab, Portuguese, Indian and other influences that have helped shape the character of Kenya's coast.

This guided city experience brings together important heritage landmarks, local neighbourhoods and scenic waterfront stops. Discover the stories behind the city's architecture while enjoying time to explore and take photographs.

  • Explore the historic Fort Jesus area
  • Walk through the characterful lanes of Old Town
  • Discover local markets and coastal trading culture
  • Visit the famous Mombasa Tusks
  • Enjoy the atmosphere at Mama Ngina Waterfront
Tour Highlights

Old Town

Your Day in Mombasa

Detailed City Tour Itinerary

The order of visits may be adjusted according to your pickup location, traffic, site access and the opening arrangements of individual attractions.

Stop 01 · Historic Landmark

Fort Jesus

Begin your heritage experience at Fort Jesus, the historic Portuguese fort overlooking the entrance to the old harbour. Learn about the fort's strategic importance and the different communities and powers that shaped Mombasa's history.

  • Explore the exterior and historic surroundings
  • Hear about the fort's history and architecture
  • Optional museum visit, subject to opening and entry arrangements

Admission and any guide arrangements inside the monument should be confirmed when booking.

Stop 02 · Local Culture

Mombasa Old Town & Local Markets

Continue into the historic Old Town, where narrow streets, balconies, traditional Swahili buildings and intricately carved doors tell stories of centuries of coastal trade.

Explore the local atmosphere and, depending on the day's route, visit a traditional market or selected shops selling fabrics, spices and locally made products.

  • Discover traditional Swahili architecture
  • Learn about the area's cultural influences
  • Optional shopping and souvenir stops
Stop 03 · Architecture & Heritage

Mandhry Mosque & Historic Streets

Admire the historic architecture around Mandhry Mosque and learn about the longstanding Islamic and Swahili heritage of Mombasa's Old Town. Your guide can explain the architectural details and the role of the surrounding neighbourhood in the city's history.

This is an exterior sightseeing stop unless permission and suitable arrangements for entry have been confirmed. Visitors should respect local customs and places of worship.

Stop 04 · City Landmark

Mombasa Tusks & Moi Avenue

Visit the famous aluminium tusks that form one of Mombasa's most recognisable landmarks. Take photographs and hear about their place in the city's public identity and history.

Continue through the surrounding city centre to observe the everyday rhythm of Mombasa.

Stop 05 · Waterfront Experience

Mama Ngina Waterfront

End the sightseeing experience at Mama Ngina Waterfront. Enjoy the open views across the Likoni Channel, watch the movement of vessels and experience one of Mombasa's popular public gathering spaces.

  • Relax along the waterfront promenade
  • Enjoy scenic views of the channel
  • Take photographs and enjoy refreshments at your own expense

After the visit, your driver will arrange the return transfer to your agreed drop-off point.

Itinerary note: This is a suggested full-day programme, not a fixed timetable. Traffic, weather, site access, visitor numbers and opening arrangements may affect the order and time spent at each stop.
