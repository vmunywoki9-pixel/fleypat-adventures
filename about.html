Fleypat Adventures

Fleypat Adventures is a tour and travel company based in Mombasa, Kenya, established on 9 April 2022 and founded by Felix Ivayo. We create travel experiences connecting visitors with Kenya’s wildlife, coastline, culture and wider destinations.

We support clients with safari planning, beach holidays, excursions, accommodation reservations, airport transfers, group travel and other travel arrangements.

Our approach is built around understanding each traveller’s needs and providing clear, practical options before a booking is confirmed.

Our Vision

To become a trusted travel partner known for memorable journeys, responsible tourism and thoughtful customer service.

Our Mission

To connect travellers with rewarding destinations through well-coordinated and customer-focused experiences.

Our Values

Integrity, hospitality, reliability, respect for communities and responsible travel.

Responsible Tourism

We encourage respectful wildlife viewing, care for natural environments, appreciation of local cultures and support for local businesses.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide clear quotations, explain inclusions, communicate changes promptly and help clients prepare for their journeys.
