Who we are

A Journey Inspired by Exploration

Fleypat Adventures is a tour and travel company based in Mombasa, Kenya. Established on 9 April 2022 and founded by Felix Ivayo, the company was built around a passion for travel, discovery and creating meaningful experiences.

Kenya is a country of remarkable contrasts. From the wildlife-rich landscapes of its national parks and reserves to the Indian Ocean coastline, historic towns and vibrant local cultures, there is something special to discover around every corner.

We help travellers explore these experiences through safari planning, beach holidays, excursions, accommodation reservations, airport transfers, group travel and other travel arrangements.

Our approach begins with understanding what each traveller wants from a journey. We aim to provide clear information, practical options and thoughtful coordination before a booking is confirmed.

Travel with respect

Our Commitment to Responsible Tourism

We believe that travel should create rewarding experiences while encouraging respect for the places, wildlife and communities that make each destination special.

Kenya's natural and cultural heritage is an important part of its identity. We encourage travellers to approach every destination with care, curiosity and respect.

  • Encouraging respectful wildlife viewing and observation.
  • Promoting care for natural environments and protected areas.
  • Respecting local traditions, cultures and communities.
  • Recognising the importance of local businesses and service providers.
  • Encouraging thoughtful and informed travel choices.
