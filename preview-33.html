Coastal Adventure

Explore the marine beauty of Mombasa

Located off the north coast of Mombasa, Mombasa Marine National Park and Reserve protects an important section of the Indian Ocean's coastal marine environment.

The park is known for its coral gardens and marine biodiversity. Depending on the sea conditions and the excursion selected, visitors can enjoy a boat trip, view the underwater environment through a glass-bottom boat, or take part in guided snorkelling.

  • Boat excursion into the marine park
  • Opportunity to observe marine habitats
  • Optional snorkelling where conditions permit
  • Coastal and ocean scenery
  • Time to relax by the beach after the excursion

Your Marine Excursion

Suggested Tour Itinerary

The exact departure time and activity sequence are arranged around tides, weather, boat availability and the chosen departure point.

Stage 01 · Departure

Meet Your Boat Crew

Arrive at the agreed beach departure point in the Nyali, Bamburi or nearby coastal area. Meet the boat crew, confirm the excursion arrangements and receive a safety briefing.

  • Meet at the confirmed departure location
  • Receive boat and activity instructions
  • Board the arranged excursion vessel
Stage 02 · Ocean Excursion

Boat Ride into the Marine Park

Travel by boat towards the marine park and enjoy the coastal views along the way. Depending on the vessel booked, you may have the opportunity to view the underwater environment through a glass-bottom section.

The crew will explain the route and the conditions expected during the trip.

Stage 03 · Marine Discovery

Optional Snorkelling & Reef Exploration

If included in your selected package and conditions are suitable, enjoy a guided snorkelling session in an authorised area. Observe the underwater environment and learn about the marine habitats.

  • Snorkelling is optional
  • Follow the crew's safety instructions
  • Use appropriate flotation equipment if needed
  • Do not touch, collect or stand on coral

Guests who prefer to remain dry can enjoy the boat experience without entering the water.

Stage 04 · Return

Coastal Relaxation & Return

Depending on the route, tide and weather, the excursion may include a suitable beach or sandbar stop. Enjoy the coastal setting before returning to the agreed landing point.

After disembarking, your transfer or onward arrangements will follow the booking confirmation.

Important: Snorkelling, sandbar stops, underwater visibility and marine-life sightings depend on weather, tides, sea conditions, safety decisions and the specific boat excursion arranged. No wildlife sighting or underwater visibility can be guaranteed.
Before You Go

Tour Information

A few practical details to help you prepare for your marine park excursion.

What to Bring

  • Swimwear and a change of clothes
  • Towel and comfortable beach footwear
  • Sun protection, sunglasses and a hat
  • Waterproof bag for personal belongings
  • Camera or phone with suitable protection
  • Personal drinking water

Safety & Marine Conservation

  • Follow the boat crew's safety briefing
  • Wear the provided life jacket when instructed
  • Tell the crew if you are not comfortable swimming
  • Never touch or stand on living coral
  • Do not feed or chase marine animals
  • Follow all park and operator conservation rules

Departure Location

Departure is arranged from a suitable beach access point, depending on the selected operator and your accommodation location.

Tides & Weather

Departure time and activity sequence may change according to tides, sea conditions and operational safety decisions.

Park Entry & Boat Charges

Applicable KWS entry fees, boat charges, equipment and activity costs should be confirmed and itemised in your quotation.
