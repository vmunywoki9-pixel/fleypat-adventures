About This Safari

This 11-day journey combines some of Kenya's most celebrated wildlife destinations with a relaxing finish at the Indian Ocean coast. Starting in Nairobi, the safari travels through the Maasai Mara, Lake Naivasha, Ol Pejeta Conservancy, Aberdare, Amboseli, Tsavo West and Tsavo East before continuing to Mombasa.

Along the way, enjoy classic African game drives, scenic landscapes, a Lake Naivasha boat excursion, a walking experience at Crescent Island, rhino conservation experiences at Ol Pejeta, the unique Aberdare tree-hotel setting and views of Mount Kilimanjaro from Amboseli when weather conditions are clear.

The journey concludes with a transfer to Kenya's coast, where you can continue your holiday by the beach.

Your journey day by day

Detailed 11-Day Itinerary

Follow the journey from Nairobi through Kenya's renowned safari destinations to the Indian Ocean coast.

Day 01

Arrival in Nairobi

Upon arrival at Jomo Kenyatta International Airport, you will be met by our representative and transferred to your Nairobi hotel. After check-in, take time to relax after your journey. Depending on your arrival time, the rest of the day can be spent at leisure.

Your safari preparations and the following day's departure arrangements can be discussed with the team.

Destination: Nairobi Accommodation: Nairobi City Hotel Meal Plan: Bed & Breakfast
Day 02

Nairobi to Maasai Mara National Reserve

After breakfast, depart Nairobi and travel towards the Maasai Mara, passing through the scenic Great Rift Valley. Stop at a viewpoint to appreciate the sweeping landscape before continuing through the countryside towards the reserve.

Arrive in the Mara in time for lunch and check in at Mara Sopa Lodge. Later, head out for your first afternoon game drive in search of the wildlife that makes this region famous.

Return to the lodge in the evening for dinner and overnight.

Route: Nairobi – Maasai Mara Activity: Afternoon Game Drive Accommodation: Mara Sopa Lodge
Day 03

Full-Day Exploration of the Maasai Mara

Spend the day exploring the Maasai Mara on game drives. The reserve's open grasslands and varied habitats provide opportunities to search for elephants, lions, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and other wildlife.

Your guide will help you explore different areas of the reserve, following wildlife sightings and local conditions. Depending on the agreed arrangements, a picnic lunch may be enjoyed during the day's exploration.

Optional activities include a Maasai village visit or a hot-air balloon safari. These are additional experiences and must be arranged separately.

Return to Mara Sopa Lodge for dinner and overnight.

Activity: Full-Day Game Drives Optional: Balloon Safari Accommodation: Mara Sopa Lodge
Day 04

Maasai Mara to Lake Naivasha

After breakfast, depart the Maasai Mara and travel towards Lake Naivasha. Enjoy the changing scenery as you leave the savannah behind and approach the Great Rift Valley lakes region.

On arrival, enjoy a boat excursion on Lake Naivasha. Look out for hippos in the water and fish eagles around the lake. Continue to Crescent Island for a guided walking experience among the wildlife found in this area.

Later, check in at Lake Naivasha Sopa Resort for dinner and overnight.

Route: Maasai Mara – Naivasha Activities: Boat Ride & Walking Safari Accommodation: Lake Naivasha Sopa Resort
Day 05

Lake Naivasha to Ol Pejeta Conservancy

After breakfast, depart Lake Naivasha and travel towards Nanyuki and the Ol Pejeta Conservancy area. The journey takes you through Kenya's central highlands towards the foothills of Mount Kenya.

Depending on the day's schedule and arrival arrangements, make an equator stop near Nanyuki before proceeding to Ol Pejeta.

Explore the conservancy's conservation attractions, including its rhino sanctuary and Sweetwaters Chimpanzee Sanctuary, subject to access arrangements and opening conditions.

Check in at Sweetwaters Serena Camp for dinner and overnight.

Route: Naivasha – Nanyuki – Ol Pejeta Activities: Rhino & Chimpanzee Conservation Accommodation: Sweetwaters Serena Camp
Day 06

Ol Pejeta to Aberdare National Park Area

After breakfast, leave Ol Pejeta and travel towards the Aberdare region. Arrive at Outspan Hotel for lunch and complete the transfer arrangements for your tree-hotel stay.

Continue by the designated shuttle to the tree hotel. The accommodation offers a distinctive forest setting overlooking a waterhole and salt lick that attract wildlife.

Spend the evening observing wildlife from the viewing areas. The waterhole may attract elephants, buffaloes and other animals, although sightings cannot be guaranteed.

Route: Ol Pejeta – Aberdare Activity: Tree-Hotel Wildlife Viewing Accommodation: Aberdare Tree Hotel
Day 07

Aberdare to Amboseli National Park

After breakfast, take the designated transfer back to Outspan Hotel and reconnect with your safari vehicle. Continue towards Amboseli, with a journey through the Nairobi area and onward towards southern Kenya.

A lunch stop will be arranged along the route. Continue towards Amboseli, arriving later in the day depending on traffic, road and transfer conditions.

Check in at Amboseli Sopa Lodge and relax after the journey.

Route: Aberdare – Nairobi – Amboseli Activity: Scenic Transfer Accommodation: Amboseli Sopa Lodge
Travel planning note: This is a particularly long transfer day. We recommend confirming the departure arrangements and considering an alternative overnight stop if a more relaxed pace is preferred.
Day 08

Full-Day Safari in Amboseli National Park

Spend the day exploring Amboseli National Park, a landscape known for its elephant herds, open plains and wetlands. When weather conditions are clear, Mount Kilimanjaro provides a spectacular backdrop to the scenery.

Game drives take you through different habitats, including the park's swamps and spring-fed areas, where wildlife gathers to feed and drink.

Look out for elephants, zebras, giraffes, buffaloes and other wildlife as you explore with your guide.

Return to Amboseli Sopa Lodge for dinner and overnight.

Activity: Full-Day Game Drives Highlights: Elephants & Kilimanjaro Views Accommodation: Amboseli Sopa Lodge
Day 09

Amboseli to Tsavo West National Park

After breakfast, depart Amboseli and travel towards Tsavo West National Park. Enjoy game viewing as you enter this diverse landscape of rolling hills, volcanic features and natural springs.

Visit the Shetani Lava Flows, an impressive landscape formed by ancient volcanic activity. Continue to Mzima Springs, a natural spring system known for its clear water and underwater viewing chamber.

Look for hippos and other wildlife around the springs before proceeding to Ngulia Safari Lodge.

Route: Amboseli – Tsavo West Activities: Shetani Lava Flows & Mzima Springs Accommodation: Ngulia Safari Lodge
Day 10

Tsavo West to Tsavo East National Park

After breakfast, depart Tsavo West and travel towards Tsavo East. Continue enjoying game viewing along the route as the landscape changes into the expansive plains and open habitats of Tsavo East.

Explore areas around the Galana River, an important watercourse in this vast ecosystem. Your guide will help you look for elephants, giraffes, zebras, antelopes and other wildlife.

Later, proceed to Voi Safari Lodge for dinner and overnight.

Route: Tsavo West – Tsavo East Activity: En-route Game Viewing Accommodation: Voi Safari Lodge
Day 11

Tsavo East to Mombasa – Journey to the Coast

After breakfast, depart Tsavo East and begin your journey towards Mombasa. Enjoy the changing scenery as you leave the national park and travel towards Kenya's coastal region.

On arrival in Mombasa, continue to your selected beach hotel or coastal accommodation. Depending on your arrival time and hotel arrangements, enjoy the remainder of the afternoon at leisure.

Your safari concludes with the opportunity to extend your holiday along the Kenyan coast.

Route: Tsavo East – Mombasa Activity: Coastal Transfer Accommodation: Beach Hotel – To Be Selected

Before you travel

Practical Information

  • Travel documents: Ensure that your passport and any required entry authorization are valid for your journey. Check the current requirements applicable to your nationality.
  • Clothing: Pack comfortable safari clothing, a light jacket, walking shoes, sun protection and suitable clothing for the different weather conditions experienced across Kenya.
  • Photography: Bring your camera, spare batteries and sufficient storage for wildlife and landscape photography.
  • Health and personal preparation: Seek appropriate travel health advice before departure and carry any personal medication you require.
  • Wildlife viewing: All wildlife sightings are natural and cannot be guaranteed. Follow your guide's instructions and respect park regulations.
  • Optional activities: Balloon safaris and cultural visits are optional and subject to separate arrangements, availability and applicable charges.
  • Road travel: Journey times can vary according to road conditions, traffic, weather and wildlife viewing stops.
  • Itinerary flexibility: The order of activities may be adjusted when necessary due to operational conditions, access restrictions or weather.
  • Accommodation: All named properties are subject to availability and confirmation at the time of booking. Any alternative will be discussed before confirmation.
