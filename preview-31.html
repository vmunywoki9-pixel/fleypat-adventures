Discover Shimba Hills

A Coastal Rainforest and Wildlife Escape

Located in Kwale County, Shimba Hills National Reserve offers a different kind of wildlife experience, combining green hills, forest habitats, open grassland and scenic views.

The reserve is especially known for its rare sable antelope, forest elephants and rich birdlife. Its landscape is a refreshing contrast to the open savannah parks found elsewhere in Kenya.

One of the area's best-known attractions is Sheldrick Falls, reached by a nature walk through the reserve's forested environment.

Important: Wildlife sightings are not guaranteed. Access to Sheldrick Falls, walking routes and swimming opportunities depends on park rules, guide instructions and current conditions.

Your Day Experience

Shimba Hills & Sheldrick Falls Itinerary

The programme below is a suggested full-day experience. The order of activities may be adjusted according to park access, road conditions, weather and the waterfall walking arrangements.

Morning · Departure

Hotel Pickup and Journey to Shimba Hills

Your day begins with pickup from your agreed hotel or meeting point in Diani, Mombasa or another confirmed coastal location.

Travel inland towards Shimba Hills National Reserve. On arrival, complete the required entry arrangements and meet your guide for an introduction to the reserve.

  • Pickup from your agreed location.
  • Scenic road transfer to Shimba Hills.
  • Park entry and safari briefing.
Morning · Wildlife exploration

Game Drive Through the Reserve

Enjoy a game drive through the reserve's open grassland, forest edges and scenic tracks. Keep an eye out for elephants, sable antelope, buffalo, giraffes and other wildlife that may be present.

Your guide will help identify animals, birds and interesting features of the reserve while choosing suitable routes for the day's conditions.

Shimba Hills is particularly recognised for its sable antelope population, although sightings cannot be guaranteed.

Midday · Guided nature walk

Walk to Sheldrick Falls

Continue to the designated starting point for the Sheldrick Falls walk. Accompanied by the appropriate guide or ranger, follow the approved trail through the forest towards the waterfall.

Enjoy the changing vegetation, forest atmosphere and sound of flowing water as you approach the falls. Take time to appreciate the natural surroundings and capture photographs.

The walk includes uneven and potentially steep sections. Allow enough time and follow the guide's advice throughout the experience.

Afternoon · Waterfall experience

Enjoy the Waterfall and Surrounding Forest

Spend time enjoying the waterfall and its natural surroundings. Depending on water conditions, safety advice and current park rules, your guide will advise whether visitors may approach the pool or enter the water.

Relax, take photographs and enjoy the peaceful environment before beginning the return walk.

Swimming is not guaranteed and should only take place when expressly permitted and considered safe.

Afternoon · Return journey

Return to the Main Reserve and Coastal Hotel

After the waterfall visit, follow your guide back along the approved trail. Depending on the agreed schedule and remaining time, enjoy a final scenic stop or refreshments.

Exit the reserve and return to your hotel or agreed drop-off point, bringing your Shimba Hills day trip to an end.

Useful Tour Information

What to Bring

  • Use approved trails and follow guide instructions.

  • Confirm park fees and guide arrangements before travel.
