Welcome to Kenya's south coast

A tropical escape along the Indian Ocean

Diani Beach is renowned for its long stretch of white sand, clear coastal waters and palm-lined shoreline. Located south of Mombasa in Kwale County, it is an inviting destination for beach lovers, couples, families and adventure seekers.

Spend your days relaxing beside the ocean, trying selected water activities, enjoying a traditional dhow experience or exploring the natural and cultural attractions found around the south coast.

Diani also makes a convenient base for excursions to Wasini Island, Kisite-Mpunguti Marine Park, Shimba Hills and other nearby attractions.

Coastal scenery and heritage

Discover the Kongo River and Kongo Mosque

At the northern end of Diani Beach, the Kongo River meets the Indian Ocean. The estuary and surrounding landscape offer a peaceful contrast to the open beach.

The historic Kongo Mosque is an important local heritage landmark. Visitors should respect local customs and confirm appropriate visiting arrangements.

  • Enjoy views of the river mouth and coastline
  • Consider a locally arranged sunset dhow cruise
  • Discover the area's cultural and historical character
Nature and conservation

Explore Diani's Natural Environment

Beyond its beaches, Diani is surrounded by coastal forests, mangrove habitats and important conservation initiatives. These experiences provide a chance to appreciate another side of the south coast.

A visit to Colobus Conservation or a guided excursion to Kaya Kinondo can add nature and cultural learning to your holiday.

  • Learn about coastal primate conservation
  • Discover local forest ecology
  • Experience guided cultural interpretation
  • Support responsible community-based tourism
Explore beyond the beach

Places to Visit Around Diani

Make your coastal holiday even more memorable with excursions to nearby marine, wildlife and cultural destinations.

Wasini Island and Kisite Marine Park

Enjoy a full-day marine excursion from the Shimoni area. Depending on conditions and the selected operator, activities may include boat trips, snorkelling and marine wildlife viewing.

Shimba Hills National Reserve

Explore coastal forest, rolling hills and wildlife habitats on a safari excursion. The reserve is also known for the Sheldrick Falls walking experience.

Funzi Island

Discover a quieter coastal environment with mangrove waterways, island scenery and a tidal sandbank experience, subject to tide conditions and tour arrangements.

Kaya Kinondo Sacred Forest

Learn about the cultural and spiritual importance of a sacred forest protected by the local Digo community. Visits should be arranged with an authorised local guide.

Chale Island

Explore a scenic island destination south of Diani, known for its coastal setting and beautiful ocean surroundings. Access and activities depend on arrangements.

Tsavo East National Park

Combine your beach holiday with a wildlife safari. An overnight or multi-day safari is a more comfortable option than attempting a rushed return day trip.

Planning a Visit to Diani Beach

Here are some useful considerations when arranging your Diani Beach holiday.

Best Time to Visit

Diani is a year-round destination. Many visitors prefer the generally drier periods for beach holidays and outdoor activities. Coastal weather can change, and rainfall is possible at different times of the year.

Water activities depend on sea conditions, visibility, wind and tides. Confirm marine excursions close to your travel date.

Getting to Diani

  • Ukunda Airstrip serves the Diani area.
  • Flights from Nairobi may be available depending on schedules.
  • Road transfers can be arranged from Mombasa.
  • Visitors arriving through Moi International Airport should allow time for the road journey and Likoni crossing arrangements.
  • Private transfers can be arranged in advance.

How Long Should You Stay?

A three- or four-day stay is a good introduction to Diani, allowing time for beach relaxation and one or two excursions.

Five to seven days provide more flexibility for marine activities, nature visits and a slower coastal holiday.

What to Pack

  • Light, comfortable clothing
  • Swimwear and beachwear
  • Sun protection and sunglasses
  • Comfortable footwear for excursions
  • A light cover-up for cultural visits
  • Personal medication and travel essentials
