Discover the Magic of Zanzibar

Zanzibar is a beautiful Indian Ocean destination known for its historic towns, Swahili culture, spice heritage and white-sand beaches. The main island, Unguja, offers a variety of experiences, from exploring old streets and traditional markets to relaxing beside turquoise coastal waters.

In Stone Town, winding streets reveal centuries of cultural exchange, distinctive architecture and a lively waterfront. Beyond the historic centre, spice farms, forest reserves, fishing villages and beach communities provide another perspective on island life.

Whether you are planning a romantic escape, cultural holiday, family break or beach adventure, Zanzibar offers plenty of ways to create a memorable journey.

What to Experience in Zanzibar

Discover the island through its history, nature, food, coastal activities and relaxed beach lifestyle.

01 · HERITAGE

Explore Stone Town

Walk through narrow historic streets, admire carved wooden doors and discover the town's markets, waterfront and historic architecture. Access to individual buildings and museums should be confirmed before visiting.

02 · LOCAL CULTURE

Visit a Spice Farm

Discover the island's spice-growing heritage and learn about cloves, cinnamon, nutmeg, vanilla and other tropical plants during a guided farm experience.

03 · NATURE

Explore Jozani Forest

Discover Zanzibar's forest environment and look for the endemic Zanzibar red colobus monkey. Sightings depend on natural conditions and animal movements.

History and Swahili heritage

Discover the Stories of Stone Town

Stone Town is the historic heart of Zanzibar City and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its architecture and street layout reflect centuries of interaction between African, Arab, Indian and European influences.

Explore the old waterfront, visit historic landmarks where open to visitors, browse local shops and experience the atmosphere of the town's traditional streets.

Coastal escapes

Explore Zanzibar's Beautiful Beaches

Zanzibar's coastline offers a variety of beach experiences, from lively northern resorts to quieter eastern shores and traditional fishing communities.

NORTH COAST

Nungwi Beach

Nungwi is known for its white sand, coastal scenery, boat activities and lively beach atmosphere. It is a popular area for swimming and seaside stays.

EAST COAST

Jambiani Beach

Jambiani offers a relaxed coastal atmosphere, long sandy stretches and a glimpse of traditional fishing and seaweed-farming communities.

NORTHWEST COAST

Kendwa Beach

Kendwa is a favourite for beach relaxation, ocean views and sunset experiences. It is an appealing option for couples and travellers seeking a coastal holiday.

Beyond the main island

Discover Zanzibar's Island Excursions

Explore nearby islands and marine environments through organised excursions, with activities arranged according to weather and local conditions.

Prison Island

Visit Changuu Island, commonly called Prison Island, for its history, coastal scenery and giant Aldabra tortoises. Boat transfers and visitor access should be confirmed.

Mnemba Atoll Area

The waters around Mnemba are known for marine life and snorkelling experiences. Access arrangements, permitted activities and conditions should be checked with the local operator.

Relax and enjoy island life

Make Time for the Indian Ocean

Zanzibar is a destination where you can slow down and enjoy the rhythm of island life. Spend time on the beach, enjoy fresh seafood, take a boat excursion or simply relax by the ocean.

Water activities can include snorkelling, diving, sailing and other guided marine experiences. Availability and suitability depend on the location, weather, tides and operator arrangements.

Flavours of the island

Experience Zanzibar's Food and Culture

Zanzibar's food culture reflects its coastal location and long history of trade. Local cuisine brings together seafood, coconut, rice, tropical fruits and aromatic spices.

Explore local markets, sample traditional dishes and discover the island's Swahili hospitality. For an authentic experience, ask your guide about local food stops and cultural experiences during your stay.

Before you travel

Planning Your Zanzibar Holiday

We can help coordinate your island arrangements, accommodation, transfers and excursions around your travel dates.

Getting There

Zanzibar can be reached by air or by ferry from mainland Tanzania. We can help discuss suitable travel connections depending on your departure point and schedule.

Best Time to Visit

Zanzibar is a year-round destination. Weather patterns vary, with drier and wetter periods influencing beach conditions and outdoor activities.

What to Bring

Pack light clothing, swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses, comfortable walking shoes and suitable clothing for visits to religious or cultural sites.

Accommodation

Choose from a range of accommodation styles, from boutique town stays to beach resorts and private coastal properties, subject to availability.

Local Transport

Airport transfers, hotel transfers and private island transport can be arranged according to your itinerary and the locations you wish to visit.

Travel Documents

Check passport validity, visa requirements, entry rules and any applicable health or travel insurance requirements for your nationality before departure.
