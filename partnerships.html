Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful answers for businesses and professionals interested in contacting Fleypat Adventures.

Which businesses can approach Fleypat Adventures?

Hotels, lodges, camps, tour operators, travel agents, transport providers, professional guides, excursion providers, corporate organisations and other relevant tourism businesses are welcome to introduce themselves.

Can a small or independent business propose a partnership?

Yes. We welcome relevant enquiries from both established organisations and independent tourism professionals. Please provide a clear introduction to your services.

How do I submit a partnership proposal?

You can use our contact page, email info@fleypatadventures.com or reach our team through WhatsApp. Include your business details and the type of collaboration you would like to discuss.

Does sending an enquiry guarantee a partnership?

No. An enquiry is an opportunity to introduce your business and discuss possible areas of collaboration. Any partnership would be subject to further discussion and agreement between the parties.

Can international travel businesses contact you?

Yes. We welcome relevant enquiries from international travel professionals and organisations interested in discussing Kenya and East African travel arrangements.

What information should I include in my proposal?

Include your organisation's name, location, website or social media links, a short description of your services, your proposed collaboration and your contact details.
